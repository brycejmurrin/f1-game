Why was the stop cheaper under VSC?

What happened in Australia?

At the 2026 Australian Grand Prix, Mercedes switched Russell and Antonelli from Mediums to Hards on lap 12 under VSC. Ferrari kept Leclerc and Hamilton out until laps 25 and 28. Mercedes finished first and second, ahead of both Ferraris.

Timing recorded Russell and Antonelli’s pit sequences at 17.794 s and 20.985 s. Relative pit loss also depends on the time taken by a car on track.

Model and rules

The timeline compares the pit path and the track path between equivalent timing markers. The fictional pit path takes 4 s at entry, 18 s in transit, 2.5 s for service and 5.5 s at exit. The finish projection uses illustrative tyre-pace and traffic costs.

Under VSC, cars slow to a minimum sector time and overtaking is generally restricted. In the race, cars can enter the pits under VSC for a tyre change. A physical Safety Car gathers the field into a queue.
