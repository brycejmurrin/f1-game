What happened in Australia?

At the 2026 Australian Grand Prix, Mercedes switched Russell and Antonelli from Mediums to Hards on lap 12 under VSC. Ferrari kept Leclerc and Hamilton out until laps 25 and 28. Mercedes finished first and second, ahead of both Ferraris.

Timing recorded Russell and Antonelli’s pit sequences at 17.794 s and 20.985 s. Relative pit loss also depends on the time taken by a car on track.

Model and rules

The replay uses constant speeds over a fictional 400 m sector. Markers in the pit lane show schematic progress through entry, transit, service and exit. The projections add stop losses, tyre pace and traffic against a shared reference race pace. The rejoin comparison models one reference car. Traffic in the last preset adds time only when you rejoin behind it. Apex’s live race strategy uses its own timing, tyre wear and track data.

Under VSC, the FIA sets minimum reference times through sectors. Overtaking is generally restricted, with stated exceptions. The race pit lane can be entered during VSC for tyre changes. A physical Safety Car forms a queue instead. The lesson's speed controls illustrate elapsed time, with no universal VSC speed limit implied.

Both dry plans here use Medium and Hard, with Hard a mandatory specification for this fixture. The real race requirement uses at least two slick specifications when intermediate or wet tyres haven’t been used, with at least one mandatory race specification.
