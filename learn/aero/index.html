Model and sources

Apex’s classic wake model uses forward gap and lateral offset. Grip is the wake multiplier relative to clean air at the same speed. Both presets hold speed at the game reference of 72 m/s (259 km/h). The straight assumes steady steering and no braking; the corner assumes enough steering to lose the tow.

Car positions are schematic. Tracers illustrate airflow. The 34 m centreline cutoff belongs to this game model.
