Bryce (2026-11-24 ~22:27Z, orchestrator chat): "always use ask user question tool to notify me if you need something".

**Why:** he follows the orchestrator from his phone. A question at the end of a long status message is easy to miss, and he often missed which reply was his (a skip-button A/B/C choice, a device-check answer). The AskUserQuestion dialog notifies him and shows the options on their own.

**How to apply:**
- This is for the ORCHESTRATOR session that Bryce steers in his own chat. When you need a decision, a click, a device step or a yes/no from him, call AskUserQuestion: 1–4 short questions, each standing alone, your pick first and marked "(Recommended)".
- Workers still follow steward §Lane 6 (`needs-decision` + one comment; no question at the end of a turn). They have no human in their chat. The orchestrator relays worker decisions to Bryce through AskUserQuestion, and posts his answer back on GitHub (his own `decision:` comment is still the grant where a label or decision line is required).
- Don't use it for status. Report merges and progress as normal text.
