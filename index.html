Menus: arrows move in every direction — Left/Right leave a list for the header or column beside it. Enter confirms. Keys always steer on a desktop. ADAPTIVE BUTTONS (default ON) slows the keys at speed. BRAKE CUE ticks faster when a corner needs the anchors — it never brakes for you
CONTROLLER
Left stick / D-pad steer. RT / A gas. LT / B brake (triggers are analog). X boost toggle. Y overtake. D-pad up aero. LB / RB gears. View cycles camera. Start pauses.
PlayStation: Cross / Circle / Square / Triangle and L2 / R2 are the same slots. Adaptive Buttons does not change the stick.
Menus: either stick / D-pad move in every direction (hold to repeat) — Left/Right leave a list for the header or column beside it. A confirms, B backs, triggers page. Start still pauses
TOUCH / MOBILE
Pause → SETTINGS → STEER cycles TILT / BUTTONS / TOUCH.
TILT — roll the phone; GAS + BRAKE sit left. GEARS: MANUAL (tilt only — BUTTONS already uses both thumbs) puts the shifter left and the pedals right. Calibrates at the lights. Allow motion when asked; deny switches to BUTTONS.
BUTTONS — on-screen arrows left, pedals right. Hold an arrow; slide the thumb back to ease. ADAPTIVE BUTTONS (default ON; 1 is OFF) makes that heavier at speed. BRAKE CUE is the tick that says when to brake.
TOUCH — drag on the track from where you put your finger (a tap does not steer). The car accelerates on its own; BRAKE stays left. No GAS pedal
⚡ BOOST
Toggle electric deploy on/off. Drains the energy bar; recharges when you lift or brake. Tapers off at very high speed — 2026 rules
⇆ ACTIVE AERO
The 2026 moveable wings. Z-MODE is the default: flaps shut, maximum downforce. Press Z (or tap AERO) in an ACTIVATION ZONE for X-MODE — the top elements of both wings rotate flat, cutting drag for a higher top speed but giving up grip. It slams shut the moment you brake or leave the zone. Costs no energy: unlike BOOST and OVERTAKE you pay for it in cornering, not charge.
Zones are fixed per circuit, like DRS boards — the chip counts the next one down in metres. A circuit with no straight worth 3 seconds gets none at all, and there the chip reads NO AERO ZONE: that is Monaco.
No proximity rule, and no first-lap wait — unlike OVERTAKE, every driver gets it on every lap in every zone, leader and backmarker alike. Prefer not to think about it? SETTINGS → ACTIVE AERO: AUTO takes every zone for you and removes the button
OVERTAKE
Within 1 s of the car ahead the OT light arms. Activate for 4 s of full-power deploy with no taper — the 2026 electrical overtaking aid that replaced DRS. Because it IS the overtaking aid it carries DRS's safety rules: nothing on the opening lap (it wakes when the leader starts lap 2) and nothing under a caution. The chip reads NO OVERTAKE when it is switched off rather than merely unarmed
GEARS
Toggle GEARS: MANUAL in SETTINGS when thumbs are free (desktop, or TILT on a phone). Shift up near the redline, down for corner exits. BUTTONS and TOUCH on a phone stay automatic so a thumb is free. The tachometer + gear indicator sit bottom-centre
🎥 CAMERA
Tap CAM or press C to cycle 13 views — CHASE, COCKPIT, TV SIDE, CINEMATIC and the rest. Hold CAM about a third of a second, or right-click it, for the picker. Your choice is remembered
⚙ SETTINGS
Pause (P or the ❚❚ button; Esc only if nothing is open) then SETTINGS for everything below — steering, gears, lighting, sound, music and HUD. BACK returns to the pause screen
🎮 CHANGE STEERING
In SETTINGS: STEER cycles tilt / buttons / touch on a phone. A keyboard and a plugged-in pad always work on desktop. ADVANCED STEERING opens STEERING & ASSISTS — presets, driving help, the racing line, ADAPTIVE BUTTONS (keys and on-screen arrows only; 1 is OFF, default ON) and BRAKE CUE (1 is OFF — a tick, never a takeover). The ADVANCED disclosure is lock, speed steer and the rest. Everything applies instantly and is saved
💡 LIGHTING
In SETTINGS tap LIGHTING TUNER for a live tuner — time of day, exposure, colour grade and more, with a real-time preview. Values are saved per track / time / weather
🔊 SOUND & HUD
In SETTINGS open MUSIC & SOUND for the music and effects switches, their volumes, and what is playing — or HIDE HUD for a clean cinematic view (the small eye icon brings it back)
TRACK LIMITS
Grass is slow. Cutting the track repeatedly earns a +5 s penalty
RACE
22 cars, five red lights, lights out. Points 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1
SEASON
All 24 circuits in calendar order — championship standings saved between visits
TIME TRIAL
Solo on track, no rivals. Set your fastest lap; your track record is saved and shown on the circuit list
CAREER
The long game. Sign for a team as a DRIVER and climb, or own the twelfth team in MY TEAM and run it. Every weekend qualifies, prize money buys parts, and the grid develops between seasons. Three save slots per mode, and each mode has its own guide inside — press CAREER MODES
QUALIFYING
Championship weekends start with one flying lap. DRIVE MY LAP to set your own time, or SIMULATE to take the modelled one — either way the sheet is the grid you start from. You cannot do both
👥 RACE A FRIEND
Two players, one race, no server. The host makes an invite code, the guest sends one back, and those two pastes ARE the connection — so anything you can send a message on will do. In the same room, scan the QR code instead. Both of you need the same version of the game

STEERING & ASSISTS

Pick a preset to set everything at once — most players never need more. Adjust the few controls below to taste, or open Advanced for the full set of knobs. Everything takes effect immediately and is saved.

How sharply the car answers your phone tilt. Higher = less tilt needed for full lock (more sensitive); lower = relaxed, bigger tilts.

STEERING

Overall steering feel, from forgiving and stable to sharp and direct.

DRIVING HELP

How much of each corner the car steers for you. Higher = easier to stay on track.

RACING LINE

Optional gentle pull toward the ideal line. Never overrides your steering.

Slows the keys and on-screen arrows at speed so a tap is a smaller correction. OFF (1) is the old snap. Does not change the stick, tilt, or a drag on the track. SPEED STEER still picks the speed that feels heavy.

A tick that speeds up when you need to brake for a corner. 1 is OFF. Higher = earlier warning (more lookahead). Takes over nothing.

How fast the whole field actually covers the track — you and the AI together. Shown as a share of reference pace: 100% is full speed, and every notch is 6% either way. Your dash and gearbox keep their full range at every setting; higher just means everyone laps quicker.

ADVANCED

TILT INPUT how phone motion becomes steering (tilt mode)

The same sensitivity as the top slider, with a finer label: how far you tilt the phone for full lock. Higher = less tilt needed; lower = relaxed, big tilts.

How steady the tilt filter holds the wheel. Higher = smoother and calmer at rest (kills hand jitter) with a touch more lag; lower = more immediate.

CAR HANDLING how steering becomes motion (all inputs)

How much of each corner the car steers for you. Higher = the car tracks the bend automatically and you just trim the line; lower = you do the work. The single biggest “easier to drive” knob.

Turn-in snappiness (shorter wheelbase). Higher = sharper, faster turn-in; lower = lazier and more stable.

Input curve. Low = gentle near centre for fine corrections; high = direct, 1:1 response. Raise it if steering feels mushy near centre.

Maximum turn angle at full lock. Higher = tighter cornering, but easier to over-rotate. (Auto-tapers at high speed.)

How much steering you keep at high speed. Higher = sharper and more responsive flat-out; lower = calmer and more stable.

DRIVER AID

Optional bias toward the line. PULL (+) eases toward the apex; PUSH (−) runs wider; OFF = pure manual. Never overrides your steering.
