Round 2 — fit first

Every suspect join from round 1 was measured with a mesh probe (each part's triangles split from its field-off build, then axis rays and edge-through-face tests against the rest of the car, on all 11 chassis) and fixed where it failed: the X-wing pylons floated 10 cm above the sidepods, the bridge-wing struts hung 3.6 cm above the front wing, the lower beam-wing tips poked out under the endplates, the monkey-seat top element stopped short of its plates, and the double-diffuser feed slots z-fought the ceiling. After the fixes no part group is left unattached on any chassis, and the 11 factory cars still build bit-identical meshes. Look changes from the round-1 asks: the monkey seat is 21 % wider and 4–6 cm higher, and the bridge wing sits 5 cm higher and further forward over the main plane. Frames marked “round 1” were not reshot (the capture budget went to the changed parts); the base frames are unchanged.
