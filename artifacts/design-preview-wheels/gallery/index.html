Fit check

node tools/car/car-fit-audit.mjs --team=mclaren --styles=<cat/id,…> (branch cursor/fit-audit-420b) · GAP / PENETRATION / ZFIGHT fail, OPEN warns

Each look is compared with the factory McLaren and with its own equivalent part, so rows the equivalent already carries are not counted against the look. The factory car itself has 67 failing rows (front wing, engine cover, brake ducts into tyres…); those belong to other lanes and are unchanged here.
