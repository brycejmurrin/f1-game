02 / THROUGH CHANGING CONDITIONS

Your selected lens, in every scene

THE IN-GAME SETTING

Your helmet.
Your lens.

On the visor development branch, use HELMET camera and open Settings → HUD & Camera → Cockpit. Select Tinted, choose a color and set its intensity. Clear keeps an untinted centre; Original in this gallery shows the existing helmet view.

A fair comparison.

One rendered view is held still while the visor settings change. The same corner, camera and exposure make small lens differences easier to judge.

Light, Medium and Strong use 12%, 18% and 24% tint. Dark lenses can make an already dark scene harder to read; compare them with Clear in the night views.
