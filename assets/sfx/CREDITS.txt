Engine sound effects
--------------------
f1_engine.mp3 (sustained F1 engine drone) is the ONE engine sample: looped and
pitched by rpm/gear at runtime (js/audio/engine.js). f1_rev.mp3, a high-rev
layer nothing loaded, was deleted on 2026-11-10; it is in the git history.

What is known and what is not (2026-11-10)
------------------------------------------
Known: the file's own ID3 tags (title, author, grouping) all read
"101soundboards.com", so it was downloaded from that site's soundboards, and the
project owner added it to the repo.
Not known: who recorded it, which car or event it is, and under what licence
101soundboards.com offered it. That site hosts user uploads and states no licence
for this clip, so no right to redistribute it is on record. Until the owner
supplies a recording with a known licence (or the original's terms), treat it as
unlicensed. It is listed in tests/data/asset-credits-unknown.json, a shrink-only
baseline that tests/unit/asset-credits.test.mjs holds.
