Recorded radio voice
--------------------
george.bin / george.json — the race engineer's clips, rendered offline by
tools/gen/voicepack.mjs with Kokoro-82M v1.0 (hexgrad), voice "bm_george",
through the ONNX export (onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX) and kokoro-js.

Licence: Apache-2.0 (model weights and voices).
  https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
  https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX
  https://www.npmjs.com/package/kokoro-js

The clips are synthetic speech, not a recording of any person. Regenerate
with: node tools/gen/voicepack.mjs --kokoro <dir with kokoro-js + ffmpeg-static>
