Recorded radio voices
---------------------
Selectable packs per race radio channel (js/audio/voice-pack.js VOICES), each
<id>.bin / <id>.json rendered offline by tools/gen/voicepack.mjs with
Kokoro-82M v1.0 (hexgrad) through the ONNX export
(onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX) and kokoro-js:

  george — the race engineer and the spotter   voice "bm_george"
  michael — alternative engineer and spotter  voice "am_michael"
  fable  — circuit intros and race commentary  voice "bm_fable"
  bella  — alternative intros and commentary  voice "af_bella"
  emma   — race control                        voice "bf_emma"
  heart  — the driving coach                   voice "af_heart"

Licence: Apache-2.0 (model weights and voices).
  https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
  https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX
  https://www.npmjs.com/package/kokoro-js

The clips are synthetic speech, not a recording of any person. Regenerate
with: node tools/gen/voicepack.mjs --kokoro <dir with kokoro-js + ffmpeg-static> --id <voice>
(after node tools/gen/voice-corpus.mjs refreshes what the channels say).
Whole calls and circuit introductions retain sentence-level delivery;
dynamic names and numbers can fall back to the channel's existing fragments.
