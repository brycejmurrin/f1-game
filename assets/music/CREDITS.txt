What is known and what is not (2026-11-10)
------------------------------------------
Known: the project owner provided all six for this game. They are NOT the
Beatscribe CC0 loops a README once credited: those (race_a.wav, race_b.wav,
night.wav) were removed, see below. The files carry no title, artist or
copyright tags (encoder tags only: Lavf / LAME).
Not known: who composed and performed each track, where it came from, and the
licence it is used under. Until the owner records that here, each row below says
"unknown" and the file is listed in tests/data/asset-credits-unknown.json, a
shrink-only baseline that tests/unit/asset-credits.test.mjs holds.

These six are the in-game playlist: they play in sequence in the menu and on
through the race, and SKIP TRACK in the pause menu jumps to the next one. Add
more by dropping files here and listing them in PLAYLIST in js/audio/engine.js.

Anything in this folder that PLAYLIST does not name is dead weight, not a
fallback — the Pages workflow stages assets/ wholesale, so it ships to every
visitor and is never fetched. Three CC0 race loops from Beatscribe's "Homebrew
Free Jingle and Fanfare Music Assets" (race_a.wav, race_b.wav, night.wav) sat
here unreferenced for exactly that reason and were removed; they are in the git
history if they are ever wanted back.

Bitrate (2026-10-24): menu.mp3 (160 kbps), song2.mp3 and song5.mp3 (192 kbps)
were re-encoded to 128 kbps CBR stereo with Bryce's approval, by
`node tools/gen/compress-audio.mjs --apply`, from the originals as committed at
dd923f85 (digests in that tool's TRACKS). The originals stay in git history.
