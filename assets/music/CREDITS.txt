Music tracks in this folder

menu.mp3   — provided by the project owner for this game.
song2.mp3  — provided by the project owner for this game.
song3.mp3  — provided by the project owner for this game.

These three are the in-game playlist: they play in sequence in the menu and on
through the race, and SKIP TRACK in the pause menu jumps to the next one. Add
more by dropping files here and listing them in PLAYLIST in js/game/audio.js.

Race loops (CC0 1.0 / public domain), from "Homebrew Free Jingle and Fanfare
Music Assets" by Beatscribe (https://github.com/Beatscribe/homebrew_vgm,
more at https://beatscribe.itch.io), downsampled to 22.05 kHz mono:
  race_a.wav  <- bs-mm-metal
  race_b.wav  <- bs-p1-ps-md
  night.wav   <- bs-cyb-city-night-nodrums  (race / night circuits)

To use your own music, drop replacement files with these names here.
