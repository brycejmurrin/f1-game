Seams, edges and shimmer: what was fixed

  • Outlines on every panel edge (frozen, metal flop, ghost pearl). Angle effects now read the smooth geometric normal, not the orange-peeled one, and switch off on hard panel creases (detected from how fast the normal changes per pixel).
  • Patterns breaking at panel joins (silver fade, flo-vis). Silver fade's dots are printed per panel plane, so the top panels get dots instead of stripes, and the dot edges are anti-aliased. Flo-vis streaks are thinner and fade to their average cover when they get too small to draw.
  • Mirror base magnifying jagged joins (burnt titanium, oil slick). Burnt moved off chrome onto satin metal; oil slick's colour now follows a smooth gradient across the body instead of one colour per facet.
  • Sparkle shimmer in motion (xirallic, JPS gold, and the flake base under metal flop). Every sparkle, grain and weave cell now fades to its average once it is under about 2 pixels, based on the real pixel size rather than distance. Two frames a camera step apart at 12 m flicker exactly as much as plain gloss: 4.41% of pixels for gloss, 4.41% xirallic, 4.35% JPS gold.
  • Not fixed, because they are out: test camo, engine-turned and chromated magnesium seams; gold foil's mirror base. Rain-soaked, race-worn and starlight sparkle got the same footprint fade anyway.

What changed, round by round

  1. Round 3 (this publish). The realism cut: 18 kept and made real finishes in all three renderers, 13 moved out. Fixed the flagged seam, edge and shimmer problems, re-shot everything kept in the phone frame, added join close-ups, a motion test and GLX parity stills. JPS gold and midnight purple got their own base colour; frozen moved to the matte base; true matte now looks flat. Code: cursor/design-preview-finishes-d2f5@fe67a4f6.
  2. Round 1 (first publish). Five prototypes: oil slick, frozen, fluoro, burnt titanium, holo flake.
  3. Round 2 (this page). Re-tuned those five against real photos: oil-film bands over dark metal, a satin sun sheen on frozen, the real titanium oxide order on burnt, sparse Lunaflair-style glitter.
  4. Added 26 finishes: 11 copied off the reference library (#65–#75: W196 aluminium, KNC bare carbon, flow-vis, FW47 carbon fade, W17 silver fade, AMR25 metallic flop and more) and 15 from three parallel design lanes (#76–#90: metals, paint effects, race condition).
  5. Fixed a TLX bug that rendered nine finishes black or white while GLX was right (TLX read stale shader values in later branches). All 31 now render on TLX.
  6. Renamed #64 holo to starlight: Codex shipped a holographic finish (#41), and #64 is now Lunaflair-style silver glitter anyway.
  7. Measured every finish against shipped gloss (share of car pixels that change) and ranked a shortlist.
  8. Not done this round: close-ups, race stills and team-livery pairs for #65–#90; WGX pixels (static and Dawn validation pass, 0 GPU errors).

Backends parity notes

These three rendered black on TLX before the fix while GLX was right. The GLX shots use a slightly wider garage camera. Parity for #76–#90 on GLX is not shot yet.

TLX is the default renderer, so every finish is judged on TLX first. GLX shots check that the WebGL2 port matches. WGX passes static validation (wgx-validate --static) only: a known SwiftShader issue renders the new finishes as plain gloss on WGX, so no WGX pixels are shown here.

Shader work needed for every pick. Each finish is one tint branch after the metal block, hand-ported to GLSL, TSL and WGSL, plus a surface id. The preview borrows ids 60–90 and remaps them onto existing surfaces, so nothing shipped changes. The Codex lane owns new ids 41+, and landing a pick means taking a real id there.
