Round 5: fewer fit failures

L21's fit audit scored the four preview bodies at 7.3–9.1 extra failing joins per car, above the bar of 7.9 (the 75th percentile across the shipped looks). Re-measured here with the same car-fit-audit on all 11 teams, the score counts each gap, penetration or z-fight row a body fails that its team's factory car does not fail as badly, averaged over the 11 cars. Each body's largest repeated rows were traced to their cause and fixed. Three are now under the 7.9 bar. Lowline is at 8.3, and most of what it has left are rows that L21 also sees on the factory cars on its integration tree.

The painted accent along the nose top ran in a straight line from the cockpit to the tip. Every concept cone bulges 27–98 mm above that line, so the accent was buried, and where it crossed the skin it z-fought: 215 cm² of flicker on the sharkfin nose, on all 11 chassis. On the four preview bodies the accent now runs over the skin in 12 cm steps from the cockpit deck to the tip.

stepnose wing arms seated on the low cone fit score 7.2 (round 4: 8.2)

The front-wing arms climb to a fixed 0.275 m, but the stepnose and lowline cones sit at 0.18 m over the wing, so 33–43 mm of each arm was buried in the nose. Under a low cone the arm now ends 25 mm under the skin, which seats its top about 15 mm in. Highnose and sharkfin keep their round-3 arms: moving those left the whole front wing hanging 3–24 mm clear of the nose on several chassis. The nose accent fix applies here too.

lowline coverCrown −0.08 → 0, wing arms seated fit score 8.3 (round 4: 9.5)

The pinched cover crown folded the engine cover onto itself: 406 cm² of z-fight on seven chassis. A flat crown removes it. The cover is still the lowest on the grid (628 mm at z −0.95), and its half-width narrows 273 → 264 mm. Of the rows left, brake ducts against the wheels (x7) and the tub against the bolsters (x11) also show on the factory cars after D4's rounding, per L21.

Round 4: size review

Bryce: "I feel like some are too big." Every new body was measured against the six shipped bodies and the team car on the McLaren chassis, by ray-casting the built mesh (design-preview/bodies/measure.mjs). A size is flagged when it is more than 8 % over the shipped envelope (the largest value among the team car and the six shipped bodies) or out of proportion with the real car it copies. Two sizes broke the 8 % rule: highnose's nose top and sharkfin's fin. Stepnose and lowline were inside the envelope but carried noses about 70 % bigger than the team car's. All four are smaller now. Where a cut broke a fit, it was reverted and the reason is given below.

Shipped bodies at the same framing

highnose noseDroop +0.240 → +0.215

Cut. The nose top was 552 mm at the wing, 8.4 % over the tallest shipped nose (wedge, 509 mm) and 158 mm over the team car. The raised nose is what makes this body, so it drops only 22 mm. It still rides 136 mm over the team nose and is now 4 % over the envelope. On the Benetton B191 the nose sits at about two-thirds of the front tyre height, and 530 mm is 74 % of ours. The pods and cover were already inside the shipped range.

sharkfin finH 0.30 → 0.13 m

Cut: this was the biggest miss. The fin's top reached 1128 mm, 12 cm above the roll hoop (1000 mm) and 17 % over the tallest shipped cover (arched, 962 mm). No real fin rises above the airbox: on the RB5 and SF70H it runs level with the hoop and drops into the rear wing. It now tops out at 958 mm, under the hoop, and the sail still lands in the wing. A pod cut (inletW +0.16 → +0.10, 20 mm a side) was tried and reverted: on mercedes it opened a 6.5 mm gap between the pod and the tub, and on ferrari it z-fought with the livery. The pods are 5 % wider than the team car's, inside the shipped range.

stepnose noseSlim 1.20 → 1.00

Nose cut, pods and cover justified. The cone under the step was 71 % deeper and 70 % wider than the team nose at the wing (275 × 392 vs 161 × 231 mm), and the F2012 cone under its step was slim. It is now 235 × 335. Cutting the pods and cover as well (airbox 1.30 → 1.10, inletH +0.10 → +0.05, coverLift +0.06 → +0.03) took 41–47 mm off the shoulders, but it left the haas mirrors 11.5 mm in the air and pushed the bolsters 56 mm into the pods. That cut is reverted. The shoulders (+99 mm over the team car) and the cover (+159 mm) stay inside the shipped range (needle, brick and arched pods reach 650 mm, the arched cover 962 mm). They can come down once the mirror mounts follow the pod top.

lowline noseSlim 1.30 → 1.00

Nose cut, pods justified. A low body should not have the fattest nose. Its cone was 278 × 396 mm at the wing (team 161 × 231), while the BT55 and RB19 both had slim noses. It is now 221 × 315. Pod cuts to inletW/waist 0.12, 0.16 and 0.18 were each tried and reverted: they left pod trim floating 4–7 mm off the pods and z-fought with the livery on five chassis. The pods stay 50–120 mm wider than the team car's, inside the shipped range (needle, brick and wedge reach 830 / 843 mm), and the low shoulders (306–317 mm) carry the look.

Round 2: what changed and why

  • Bryce asked for "more accurate or distinct", so each body was pushed toward its real car and away from the nearest shipped body.
  • highnose: nose raised from +0.17 to +0.24, longer and slimmer (side 3.2 → 4.1 %).
  • sharkfin: the fin is taller (0.30 m) and thicker (45 mm, new finW), and the pods are wider (3.0 → 3.5 %).
  • stepnose: the step is now 20 cm deep and 6 cm ahead of the nose root, with a longer, fatter cone (2.4 → 3.8 %).
  • lowline: wider, lower pods and a smaller airbox.
  • zeropod: the pods are lower as well as narrower. It still doesn't hold up from the side.
  • Added real-car photos beside each design, a wider side shot (the review camera cuts off the nose), close-ups, and phone frames for every design.

Ranked shortlist

  1. highnose: the strongest. It differs most from every shipped body in side view (4.1 %), and the raised nose shows at phone size. In round 3 its pylons became the wing's own arms, run up into the nose.
  2. sharkfin: the tall fin reads as a plate from the side and as a spike at phone size. It is the only body that changes the car's top line behind the halo. Round 3 made the blade thin and ran it back into the rear wing.
  3. stepnose: round 2 made the step deeper and the cone longer (side 2.4 → 3.8 %). Clear in the side and nose shots, subtle in the phone 3/4.
  4. lowline: a low engine cover and wide, low pods. Clear in side view (3.7 %), but at phone size it is close to the team car.
  5. zeropod: dropped in round 3. Its side view stayed at the floor of the shipped range (2.3 %) and it was hard to pick out at phone size. Round 2's frames are kept under Earlier rounds.
