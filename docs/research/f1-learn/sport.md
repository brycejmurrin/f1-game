# F1 sporting rules and race-day decisions

Research checked 23 November 2026. Base the lessons on [2026 FIA Section B, Issue 09](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf) and [2026 FIA Section A, Issue 03](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_a_general_provisions_-_iss_03_-_2026-06-25.pdf). Section A covers championship scoring, governance and adjudication. Section B covers how competitions run. The International Sporting Code and its appendices also apply. Event notes and binding FIA documents can supply local operating details. Use the FIA index to find the edition and check amendments or effective dates. Use Formula 1 and Pirelli explainers for accessible language and examples. [FIA regulations index](https://www.fia.com/regulations/category/110)

## Learn the weekend

- A standard competition runs FP1, FP2, FP3, Qualifying and the Grand Prix. A Sprint competition runs FP1 and Sprint Qualifying on the first day, Sprint and Grand Prix Qualifying on the second, and the Grand Prix on the third. Sprint Qualifying sets the Sprint grid. Grand Prix Qualifying sets the basis for the Grand Prix grid. Actual dates and times vary by event. [Formula 1 weekend guide](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-beginners-guide-to-the-formula-1-weekend.5RFZzGXNhEi9AEuMXwo987) · [2026 Las Vegas timetable V3](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_las_vegas_grand_prix_-_timetable_v3.pdf)
- Practice lets teams compare setups, tyres and fuel loads, so a lap-time ranking is weak evidence of race pace by itself. Qualifying uses a driver’s fastest valid lap in each segment. The 22-car 2026 field goes 22 → 16 → 10, with previous times reset for advancing cars. Grand Prix Q1/Q2/Q3 last 18/15/13 minutes and SQ1/SQ2/SQ3 last 12/10/8 minutes. Identical valid times in the same segment rank in the order they were set. Penalties and pit-lane starts can change the grid. [FIA Section B, B2.1–B2.5](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf) · [Formula 1 Sprint guide](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-beginners-guide-to-the-f1-sprint.55yJBEiF7vYkZEwSV9lZJ9)
- The scheduled Grand Prix distance ordinarily uses the fewest complete laps exceeding 305 km, or 260 km at Monaco. A Sprint uses the fewest complete laps exceeding 100 km. The race has a two-hour running limit, with suspensions added up to a three-hour total and a specified lap-end procedure. Sprint limits are one hour running and 90 minutes total. A car can retire and still classify if it completes at least the 90% threshold, rounded down to a whole lap, measured against the winner. [FIA Section B, B2.3 and B2.5](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf)
- Parc fermé restricts permitted work and adjustments. It generally begins when the car first leaves the pit lane in the relevant qualifying session, or at the end of Q1 or SQ1 if it never leaves. Sprint weekends have separate Sprint Qualifying-to-Sprint and Qualifying-to-race windows. Teams can still perform prescribed or authorized work. Use Appendix B2 and B3.5 for teaching exact permissions. [FIA Section B, B3.5 and Appendix B2](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf)

**Interaction.** Sort session cards into the two schedules and connect each qualifying session to its grid. Delete a fastest lap for a track-limit infringement, resolve two equal remaining times, then apply a grid penalty and show the difference between qualifying classification and starting grid. FIA Las Vegas Docs 48 and 53 supply a comparison with several real power-unit-related grid penalties. [FIA Las Vegas document archive](https://www.fia.com/documents/championships/fia-formula-one-world-championship-14/season/season-2026-2072/event/Las%20Vegas%20Grand%20Prix)

## Score the championships

- Full Grand Prix points by position are 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 1. Sprint points are 8 through 1 for the first eight. Drivers accumulate their own points and the constructor receives the points scored by both its cars. The 2026 season-end countback proceeds through race wins, second places and further race placings, then qualifying results if needed. [FIA Section A, A2.1–A2.2](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_a_general_provisions_-_iss_03_-_2026-06-25.pdf)
- Both scoring tables require the leader to complete at least two complete consecutive laps without SC/VSC. For a Grand Prix, the reduced points bands are under 25% (6, 4, 3, 2, 1), 25% to under 50% (13, 10, 8, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1), and 50% to under 75% (19, 14, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 3, 2, 1). At 75% or more, full Grand Prix points apply. A Sprint needs at least 50% for the standard Sprint scale. Use final classifications for the score. [FIA Section A, A2.2](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_a_general_provisions_-_iss_03_-_2026-06-25.pdf)
- History switches should expose the rules prevailing in that season. The top-10 scale began in 2010. The 2019–24 bonus point went to the fastest race lap when set by a top-10 finisher, and F1 removed it in 2025. Sprints began in 2021 with top-three scoring and originally set the Grand Prix grid. The top-eight scale arrived in 2022, a separate Sprint Qualifying session arrived in 2023, and the currently familiar session order arrived in 2024. [F1 points history](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/how-many-world-championship-titles-would-f1-drivers-have-won-using-the.182RC42vohx8STIpJR5o26) · [F1 Sprint history](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-beginners-guide-to-the-f1-sprint.55yJBEiF7vYkZEwSV9lZJ9)

**Interaction.** Give one team P2/P3 and another P1/P11. The winner’s team scores 25 and the paired podium scores 33. Let the learner add Sprint results and drag a shortened-race distance slider over thresholds. A live ticker should show positional movement separately from official scoring. Use the 2026 Las Vegas final classification to show a classified DNF and a recorded fastest lap without championship points. [FIA Las Vegas final classification](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_las_vegas_grand_prix_-_final_race_classification.pdf)

## Read tyres and pit stops

- Pirelli’s 2026 slick range runs from C1 hardest to C5 softest. The nominated trio at a given event is labelled Hard (white), Medium (yellow), Soft (red). Intermediate (green) and Full Wet (blue) address wetter conditions. C3 can be Hard at Las Vegas and Soft at Qatar, a concrete way to teach the difference between absolute compound code and event-relative label. Softer compounds commonly trade higher immediate grip for shorter life, with setup, tyre temperature, track characteristics and use all affecting the result. [Pirelli 2026 range](https://press.pirelli.com/pirelli-reveals-2026-f1-tyres-a-fresh-logo-design-and-new-compounds/) · [Pirelli final three nominations](https://press.pirelli.com/tyre-compounds-selected-for-the-final-three-races-of-the-season/) · [Monaco tyre colour guide](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/what-tyres-will-the-teams-and-drivers-have-for-the-2026-monaco-grand-prix.6RNlF5Skp9niWsEz6urQMr)
- Unless the driver used intermediates or full wets during the Grand Prix, the driver must use at least two distinct slick specifications, at least one of which is a mandatory race specification. The rule concerns specifications used during the race. Two fresh sets of the same compound count as one specification. A suspension that ends the race makes the rule’s sanction 30 seconds when applicable. There is no equivalent two-compound requirement for the Sprint. In dry Sprint Qualifying the rules limit SQ1 and SQ2 to one new medium set each and SQ3 to one soft set. The supplier nominates the mandatory race and Q3 specifications and allocations can vary with event format or tyre tests. [FIA Section B, B6.1–B6.3](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf)
- The Monaco three-set experiment applied in 2025 only. In the 2026 race, the standard rule applied and a late Safety Car prompted many teams to make another stop because the time loss was lower. [2025 Monaco rule explainer](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/explained-what-is-the-new-two-stop-rule-for-the-monaco-grand-prix-and-how.1qropbdmR05S3BqgYsHtpE) · [2026 Monaco race report](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/antonelli-secures-brilliant-victory-in-chaotic-monaco-grand-prix-amid-multiple-shock-retirements.27e644586K83z0NZd6efsz)

**Interaction.** Let learners build a stint plan from an event’s actual nominated compounds. Give the same fictional driver old-medium and fresh-hard lap curves plus a pit-lane time loss. Add traffic and cold-tyre out-lap options to show when stopping first to undercut or staying out to overcut gains position. Change the same case to VSC, SC, then rain and ask whether the stop or the compound requirement changed. Label tyre performance and time loss as representative model inputs.

## Recognize race-control states and review decisions

| Signal or state | Driver response and race effect |
| --- | --- |
| Green | Local danger has passed or normal running can resume. |
| Single yellow | Slow down, do not overtake and be ready to change direction. |
| Double yellow | Slow significantly, do not overtake and be ready to change direction or stop. |
| Virtual Safety Car | Field slows to a time delta communicated through FIA systems. No routine overtaking. Gaps tend to be broadly preserved. |
| Safety Car | Field forms a queue behind the physical car, compressing gaps. No routine overtaking, with controlled exceptions. |
| Red | Session suspended. Slow down, avoid overtaking and follow the designated stopping route. A restart can be standing or rolling, or the race may end. |
| Blue | In a race, a driver about to be lapped should facilitate the pass. Other sessions and pit exit use it differently. |
| Chequered | Session finished. The start signal, control line and classification rules determine the official result. |

SC and VSC laps count in normal race running. A red flag may change the effective time limit and resumption procedures. Pit-lane permissions and overtaking exceptions are prescribed by state. A common broadcast strategy shorthand is that pitting under SC/VSC may cost less relative to the slowed field. Queue position, traffic and current pit and event conditions can change the advantage. [FIA Section B, B5.12–B5.16](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf) · [F1 flags guide](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-beginners-guide-to-formula-1-flags.T5DqOqbWI6S4Va8Y5yMld)

Race Director and Race Control manage sessions, starts, suspension and SC/VSC deployment. Marshals assist and signal around the circuit. Stewards independently decide referred matters based on rules and evidence. The Technical Delegate and scrutineers inspect compliance and can report findings. Penalties can affect time, grid position, classification or a driver’s super-licence. Driving Standards and Penalty Guidelines support consistency and carry no regulatory force. Timing screens, provisional outcomes, final classification and appeal/review channels represent different procedural stages. [FIA Section B, B1.3, B1.5 and B1.9](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf) · [FIA Section A, A7](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_a_general_provisions_-_iss_03_-_2026-06-25.pdf) · [FIA 2026 stewards guidelines](https://api.fia.com/news/updated-fia-formula-one-world-championship-stewards-guidelines-driving-standards-penalties)

**Interactions.** Animate cars on a simple track under green, VSC and SC with time gaps displayed. Switch to red and show suspension procedures and possible restart branches. For a steward case, reconstruct a fictional side-by-side corner showing white lines, car positions, onboard perspective and retained advantage. Ask which evidence supports a finding, then reveal the actual reasoning in 2026 Las Vegas Doc 54 involving Hamilton and Norris at Turns 7–8. For penalties and retirement, Doc 61 shows a 10-second time penalty converted into a five-place grid drop at the next race in which the driver participates. Present that as a documented case with discretion. [FIA Doc 54](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_las_vegas_grand_prix_-_infringement_-_car_44_-_leaving_the_track_and_gaining_an_advantage.pdf) · [FIA Doc 61](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_las_vegas_grand_prix_-_infringement_-_car_11_-_causing_a_collision_with_car_51.pdf)

## Version-sensitive cases worth building into the content model

- Store the season, FIA section/issue and article, effective scope, event or session, and verification date for each rule. Look up event documents for official timetables, event notes, penalties and classifications. The FIA index already publishes a separate 2027 Sporting edition, and Issue 09 contains later-year changes. Treat announcements as context and published rules as the basis for a lesson.
