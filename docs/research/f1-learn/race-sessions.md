# Learn F1 through race decisions

Research checked 23 November 2026. Each session starts with a decision, shows its consequence, then lets the player rerun one changed condition. The rules reference is FIA 2026 Section B Issue 09, dated 1 October, and Section A Issue 03, dated 25 June. Historical panels cite the event evidence. March Australia supplies the observed strategy example and the playable model uses the cited 2026 rules for its own scenario. [FIA regulations index](https://www.fia.com/regulations/category/110) · [2026 Section B](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf) · [2026 Section A](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_a_general_provisions_-_iss_03_-_2026-06-25.pdf)

## 1. Box under Virtual Safety Car

**Decision.** A VSC has just been deployed and your car is approaching pit entry. Do you box for hards now or stay out on mediums and stop later? Play as the strategist for one fictional car in a 58-lap race. Pause before the pit-entry commitment point. Show the current compound, predicted stint life, relative pit cost and VSC state on the same screen. Keep the actual time of the green signal hidden until the player commits.

**Event debrief.** In Australia, George Russell and Kimi Antonelli changed from mediums to hards on the first VSC and shared a pit box in quick succession. Official timing records both stops on lap 12, with pit-summary durations of 17.794 s and 20.985 s. Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton stayed out at that point and pitted on laps 25 and 28. They finished third and fourth behind the Mercedes pair. The pit-summary durations describe timing through the pit sequence. Relative pit cost depends on how long the car on track takes to cover the equivalent segment and therefore can be lower under VSC even when pit transit takes the same time. The official analysis estimates about ten seconds of relative saving for the first VSC in this event. [Australian GP pit-stop summary](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/races/1279/australia/pit-stop-summary) · [Australian GP result](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/races/1279/australia/race-result) · [Formula 1 strategy analysis](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/monday-morning-debrief-could-ferrari-have-won-in-australia-with-a-different.6yQwaJxaJCZdS16fS2jcIw)

**Playable fixture.** Use illustrative projected losses relative to a shared reference pace. Keep the baseline and both counterfactuals visible as a waterfall after committing.

| Input | Baseline |
| --- | ---: |
| Lap and distance | Lap 12 of 58 |
| Car behind at the pit-entry decision marker | 14.0 s back |
| Green pit loss | 22.0 s |
| Full VSC pit loss | 12.0 s |
| Partial VSC pit loss | 17.0 s |
| Box now on hards, cumulative remaining-lap pace cost | 14.0 s |
| Stay out and stop on lap 25, cumulative remaining-lap pace cost | 1.0 s |
| Dry compounds | Started on medium, hard is a nominated mandatory race compound |

An equivalent timing segment takes 30 s via the pit lane in all three teaching cases. On-track elapsed time is 8 s under green, 18 s under full VSC and 13 s under partial VSC. Relative pit loss is therefore 22, 12 or 17 s. These are model inputs. Pace costs exclude the pit loss and are charged once. Compare rejoin position with an opponent who stays out at the same marker. Use projected loss at the finish for strategy comparison and avoid assigning finish positions without simulating the rest of the field.

| Replay setup | Box now | Stay out | Expected teaching result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| VSC remains active through the segment, baseline tyre life | 12 + 14 = 26 s | 22 + 1 = 23 s | Boxer rejoins 2 s ahead of the reference opponent. Staying out wins the projection by 3 s. |
| VSC remains active, hards hold pace longer | 12 + 5 = 17 s | 22 + 1 = 23 s | Boxing wins by 6 s with the same rejoin gap. |
| VSC ends mid-segment, hards hold pace longer | 17 + 5 = 22 s | 22 + 1 = 23 s | Boxer rejoins 3 s behind. Boxing wins by 1 s before traffic. |
| Same partial VSC with 3 s traffic cost behind opponent | 17 + 5 + 3 = 25 s | 22 + 1 = 23 s | Staying out wins by 2 s. |

Baseline full-VSC break-even is a box-now pace cost of 11 s, and partial-VSC break-even is 6 s before traffic. Give equal projections equal credit. A learner can choose a sound action under uncertainty and still get an unfavorable outcome. Score the reasoning separately from the modeled result. An optional late-choice branch has VSC end before the car reaches the timed segment, making the stop a 22 s green stop. Once the player crosses the commitment marker, edits require a replay.

**Controls and checkpoints.**

1. Select **Box now** or **Stay out**, then choose which variable drove the call. Let the player step one marker at a time or play forward, with **Replay from pit entry** restoring the initial state.
2. Teach VSC as sector minimum time and generally restricted overtaking. Race Control sends a VSC ending signal and then green 10–15 seconds later. Under race VSC the pit lane can be entered for a tyre change. Keep the lesson’s simplified pit commitment marker distinct from regulatory safety-car lines. [FIA Section B, B5.12](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf)
3. Validate that any dry plan uses two specifications, including at least one mandatory race specification. **Stay out all race** on mediums fails the dry tyre rule. A replay in which intermediates are actually used during the race removes that dry-compound requirement. Display the difference between changing the weather forecast and using the tyre. [FIA Section B, B6.3.6](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf)
4. Before the timing reveal, ask which field state gathers cars into a physical queue. **Safety Car** is the correct choice. **VSC** broadly preserves gaps subject to pit stops, position and driver timing differences. In a watch-only comparison, show the restart queue and keep the VSC cost model out of the physical Safety Car replay. [FIA Section B, B5.12–B5.13](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf)

**Fixture and evaluator.** Ship fixture IDs for default, long-life hards, partial VSC and partial VSC with traffic in that sequence. Store the input times as integer milliseconds and compound choices as specifications. Inputs are pit-path segment durations, on-track duration for the chosen state, initial gap behind, later green stop cost, remaining-lap pace losses for both decisions, and queued traffic cost. Compute relative pit loss as total pit path minus track path, the boxing rejoin margin as initial gap minus relative loss, and each finish projection as its own pit loss plus its pace and traffic charges. A positive rejoin margin means ahead and zero means level at the teaching marker. A projected tie leaves both options viable. Keep the chosen branch and elapsed marker in dialog state. ## 2. Build the grid from qualifying

**Decision.** Run qualifying, record valid times, then publish the starting grid. Use three columns named **Qualifying**, **Penalties** and **Starting grid**. On narrow layouts use sequential steps with an editable return path to the previous step. In a Sprint weekend the player must choose which grid they are building before starting.

**Worked qualifying fixture.** Use a fictional dry Q2 segment. Eight cars have valid best times faster than 1:34.900. Two more have valid best times of 1:34.900, with Car 9 setting it at 6:10 elapsed and Car 17 at 8:40. Car 41 posts a 1:34.830 that the stewards delete and has a standing 1:34.930. Five other cars are slower than that backup. Car 9 qualifies ninth in Q2, Car 17 tenth and Car 41 eleventh and eliminated. The 10 cars advancing to Q3 start with clear times. Car 17 can beat Car 9 in Q3 even though Car 9 set the equal Q2 time earlier. If the deleted time becomes valid on a fictional replay, Car 41 is ninth, Car 9 tenth and Car 17 eleventh. [FIA Section B, B2.4](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf)

**Event reveal.** Las Vegas qualifying had Antonelli first with a 1:33.893 and Verstappen second on 1:34.151. A five-place power-unit penalty applied to Antonelli. The FIA final grid put Verstappen P1 and Antonelli P6. Use the full final grid document when displaying the remaining cars because several other penalties applied. Show the headline as two factual statements, “Antonelli fastest in qualifying” and “Verstappen starts from pole.” [FIA final qualifying classification](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_las_vegas_grand_prix_-_final_qualifying_classification.pdf) · [FIA final starting grid](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_las_vegas_grand_prix_-_final_starting_grid.pdf)

**Controls and checkpoints.**

1. **Invalidate lap**, **Reinstate fictional lap**, **Advance session**, **Apply grid adjustment**, and **Compare with official grid**. Record times as integer milliseconds with a separate time-of-setting or order field for ties.
2. Qualifying and Sprint Qualifying in the 2026 22-car format both go 22 → 16 → 10. Qualifying segment clocks are 18/15/13 minutes, Sprint Qualifying clocks 12/10/8. Sprint Qualifying feeds the Sprint grid and Grand Prix Qualifying feeds the Grand Prix grid. Keep the Sprint result from changing the Grand Prix grid.
3. Test invalidation with and without a valid backup. With no Q2 time, the no-time ranking is a distinct rule path and stays outside the worked fixture. Compare results against the published classification and grid for the historical replay. The fictional grid adjustment can be a single penalty to keep the reasoning transparent. The broader multi-penalty process is defined separately for both grids. [FIA Section B, B2.2–B2.5](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf)

## 3. Decide a steward case and score the result

**Decision.** A car slowed noticeably on the straight while the physical Safety Car was deployed. Rule on whether the slowdown was justified. Show article B5.13.2, the radio and timing summary, comparative speed trace, locations of trailing cars, and the driver and team hearing summary if documented. Represent the case with simple diagrams and source links. Full official telemetry and audio are outside the public decision packet. [FIA 2026 Sporting Regulations, B5.13.2](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss_09_-_2026-10-01.pdf) · [FIA Las Vegas Doc 64](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_las_vegas_grand_prix_-_infringement_-_car_6_-_driving_unnecessarily_slowly.pdf)

**Worked outcome.** The Las Vegas stewards concluded that Hadjar deliberately slowed between Turns 13 and 14 to create a pit-box gap for a double-stack. They considered evidence that the speed difference to his teammate reached about 100 km/h and the trailing drivers had to slow. They imposed 10 seconds, citing the evidence, precedent and penalty guidelines. The provisional classification showed Hadjar P14 at 1:28:16.112, 0.100 s ahead of Lawson. Adding 10.000 s gave Hadjar 1:28:26.112 and reversed the order to Lawson P14, Hadjar P15. Both remain outside the points. [FIA Doc 64](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_las_vegas_grand_prix_-_infringement_-_car_6_-_driving_unnecessarily_slowly.pdf) · [FIA provisional classification](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_las_vegas_grand_prix_-_provisional_race_classification.pdf) · [FIA final classification](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_las_vegas_grand_prix_-_final_race_classification.pdf)

**Controls and branches.** Use **Inspect evidence**, **Request more evidence**, **Record finding** and **Reveal official decision**. The coaching outcome for the actual facts should require the player to connect the slowdown to its apparent tactical purpose and effect on following cars. Give a fictional replay with an obstruction confirmed on the racing surface. Slowing for that hazard supports a different finding even with a comparable speed drop. In an evidence-light branch, radio or telemetry alone could be ambiguous. The player can seek the missing evidence before choosing a finding. Label evidence diagrams as reconstructions. Use the published penalty as authoritative for the historical case and avoid treating 10 seconds as an automatic outcome for any low-speed incident. [FIA 2026 stewards guidelines](https://api.fia.com/news/updated-fia-formula-one-world-championship-stewards-guidelines-driving-standards-penalties)

**Scoreboard phase.** Move from **Provisional** to **Final** after the post-race decision. In Las Vegas, Norris P2 and Piastri P3 give McLaren 18 + 15 = 33. Verstappen P1 and Hadjar P15 give Red Bull 25 + 0 = 25. Lawson records the race’s fastest lap and takes zero championship points. Alonso’s 49 of 50 laps are enough for a classified retirement and no points in P19. A fictional cross-points-boundary replay puts one car 0.100 s ahead of the next at P10 and P11 among same-lap finishers, with P12 over ten seconds behind P10. If the P10 car takes an added 10 seconds it drops to P11, and the one point moves to the car newly in P10. A fantasy score or historical fastest-lap point should never enter the 2026 championship calculation. [FIA final classification](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_las_vegas_grand_prix_-_final_race_classification.pdf) · [FIA championship points](https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_las_vegas_grand_prix_-_championship_points.pdf) · [FIA Section A, A2.2](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_a_general_provisions_-_iss_03_-_2026-06-25.pdf)

**Shortened race branch.** Use a fictional 60-lap Grand Prix with at least two consecutive complete laps free of SC/VSC by the leader. Let the learner set the official completed laps at 14/15, 29/30 and 44/45 and check that first place receives 6/13, 13/19 and 19/25 respectively. Disabling the minimum-lap condition must produce zero even at 45 completed laps. Add a 20-lap Sprint variant with 9/10 laps and the same condition. First place receives 0/8. Toggling the shortened event should preserve the chosen finishing order. [FIA Section A, A2.2](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_a_general_provisions_-_iss_03_-_2026-06-25.pdf)

## Common presentation checks

- Keep actions available by keyboard and touch, with a visible focus state and text labels on flags, tyres and race states. An editable numeric field or adjacent buttons should provide the same control as any graph slider.
- Advance animations by discrete step or reduced-motion playback. Announce a concise event summary and avoid reading the timing table on every animation frame.
- Give each historical fixture an event date and document references. Give each modeled branch its assumptions beside the result. Replaying the same inputs should reproduce the same ranking, cost and score. The saved decision should retain the rule year, fixture version, player choice and result.
