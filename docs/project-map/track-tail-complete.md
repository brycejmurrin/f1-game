## Build and ownership map

`TrackMesh` owns the running-surface geometry and the runtime banking/kerb queries. `bankingProfile` smooths curvature, resolves curated turn anchors directly, compensates fractional anchors with the scenery shift/mirror, reseats loose straight anchors against unclaimed apexes, combines overlapping zones by greatest lift, and relaxes opposite-sign transitions through reduced lift. `bankOffsetAt` pivots the cross-section about the centreline; it clamps beyond either edge. `bankNormalAt` derives a normal from the actual neighbouring banked rail positions and cross-slope. Road and kerb geometry share the pivot.

Kerb quads are unioned per side before emission. Stripe boundaries emit coincident rings of opposite colours and skip the zero-area connecting quad, preserving hard stripes at 1.6 m despite approximately 4 m road nodes. Kerb ride maps remain per-node binary arrays. The road has fourteen columns, visual fold clamps, deterministic surface tint/wear from the baked racing line, a duplicate seam ring whose arc is `total`, verge skirts and pit-entry hatching. Those visual fold clamps do not change the driving half-width. Terrain uses TrackSurface rails and sequential clip rules against nearby roads; candidate sorting preserves the mutation-dependent clip order. Terrain normals accumulate area-weighted faces, then normalize upward. The floor is a large double-wound slab at the surface floor.

`gridSlot` supplies the slot arc/lateral model shared with cars. Grid boxes append to the start-line decal. Pit tarmac and paint consume TrackPit bands/row data: `laneVert` and `laneAt` define the drawn ribbon, and `pitPaint` cuts strips at every crossed node and lifts them above it. Pit entry hatch geometry instead appends to the unbiased road buffer. Bay-less complexes paint their boxes across the fast lane; bay complexes use the working lane.

`TrackBuildProps.buildSteps` is the orchestration boundary. It creates Float64 geometry accumulators, separate glass/water buffers, a local 6 m CSR terrain sampler, road/pit guards, the primitive/model graph, semantic prop registry, driving boundary arrays, posts, solid-segment and oriented building-mass indexes. Primitive wrappers validate dimensions, run guards, and support tri-state dry-run/replay verdicts. The registry aggregates nearby raw primitive runs, measures named records from subsequent emission, and caps records. It is diagnostic world description rather than collision truth.

Driving constraints, foliage occupancy and building occupancy are separate structures. `recordBarrier` tightens lateral limits and indexes geometry; `indexBarrier`/`indexSolid` reserve scenery geometry without changing those limits. The barrier grid remains current through incremental insertion. Building masses use a separate oriented-rectangle SAT test. `reserveEmittedSolid` projects actual emitted vertices, including overhangs, into a horizontal frame before reserving them. Thin placed decals do not create driving walls. Gantry legs use two-sided post footprints instead of closing off all runoff behind them.

The scenery transform layer distinguishes authored nodes, source/racing fractions, reversed sides and shift-only legacy helpers. `along` and `every` return authored-frame callbacks to prevent a second shift when circuits call wrapped emitters. `bakedModel` has a separate wrapper because its first argument is an asset ID. `frameAt`/`overheadSpan` receive mirror compensation where required; ground patches, grounded segments and CircuitKit keep their documented shift-only behavior. `drapeKit` is composed from the final wrapped API. These distinctions are intentional compatibility contracts, not interchangeable transforms.

Emission order is material: generic barriers/signs and theme city dressing precede the circuit callback; the venue pass follows it; queued forest edges and generic roadside trees wait until occupancy reservations are complete; lamps, bridge supports, baked-model flush, registry finalization and the engine-owned pit complex follow. TrackPit then opens the driving boundary, and three feather passes smooth boundary cliffs while protecting the pit-side keep-open nodes. Cooperative steps yield between complete phases; no claim is made that a long individual scenery callback yields internally.

## Scenery module responsibilities

- `TrackGraph` records canonical primitive recipes once, replays them through guarded/raw emitters, validates whole-object placements where requested, and records placed transforms. Full placements can withhold fused triangles when the backend supports instancing. Partial placements and nonuniform radial scales fuse. `batches({instancedOnly:true})` selects only withheld geometry, preventing glass panes already emitted to the reflective mesh from drawing twice. Signed scales and left-handed bases select reflected canonical variants with corrected winding. Baked asset meshes are compacted to referenced vertices and placed with uniform scale/yaw. Graph statistics are capability accounting, not necessarily resident GPU cost.
- `TrackModels` supplies growable accumulators and sealing, geometry validation, atomic model groups, overhead spans, water surfaces, draped ground patches, grounded segments and terrain-fitted drape cells. Model groups test both declared and emitted footprints and record bounds escapes; trial rejections omit suppression records. Overhead spans intentionally cross the road, enforce clearance/support rules, optionally stage a soffit, and can name the countdown-light gantry. Grounded segment pairs densify independently and yield chord-by-chord to pit ownership. Drape cells fit a quantized terrain plane and separate overlap planes on a height lattice.
- `CircuitKit` validates IDs, fractions, sides, gaps, sizes, frames and budgets before staging reusable facilities through modelGroup. Kit callbacks fail closed on exceptions. A pit building superseded by engine bays is a successful no-op with an explicit non-required suppression record. Hospitality, race control, cranes, shelters, recovery bays, service compounds, signs and pedestrian bridges use the same staging model.
- `SceneryNature` owns terrain-aware anchors (0.3 m embed), vegetation, canopy clearance, tree spot/facet/part occupancy, landscape masses, grandstands and spectator banks. Gap anchors use banked road height before the terrain ribbon; other anchors prefer drawn terrain. Pines and opt-in lobed broadleaves use whole graph recipes. Ordinary broadleaves and palms can relocate outward up to twelve metres to clear occupied ground, with additional part/terrain checks. Deferred forest edges see completed barrier reservations. Grandstand seating must emit before dependent shells/roofs, and terrain extensions support roofs, banks and distant ridges.
- `SceneryStructures` owns wrap-safe node walking, containment and tagged seam deduplication, barriers, fencing, gantries, marshal light panels, cameras, sponsors, stand tiers, signs and the wheel. Walks pass actual quantized spacing. Curve-adapted tiers size and aim rows from neighbour chords and cap hairpin growth. Fences reserve scenery without changing driving limits. Gantries intentionally cross tarmac via overheadSpan; marshal panel records identify dynamic flag display positions.
- `SceneryCity` owns facade materials/details, buildings and tower archetypes, street frontage, houses, motorhomes, billboards and lamps. Detail boxes entirely under sampled terrain are omitted. Unlit window panes target the glass mesh; lit panes stay in props. Wall masses must land before dependent facade sections; occupied building footprints make later masses yield. Model keys include geometry/material/side inputs where the recipe uses them. Motorhome reservations use emitted extents.
- `SceneryIdentity` composes portals, mast banks, LED facades, canyons/canopies, runoff, banked kerbs, bowl seating, broadcast compounds and pastel rows. Over-road roofs use intentional raw/overhead paths while supports remain guarded. Broadcast trucks, dishes and masts sample their own terrain and reserve the geometry actually emitted.
- `SceneryPits` draws only from the pit model: swept platform/wall profiles, approach boards, entry/exit signals, exact-arc bay placement, per-team GarageScene meshes, facade/hospitality/canopy slices, light records, team atlas quads and optional folded architectural roof. Atlas quads are ordered fascia, boards, wall panels, crests; green entry signal quads live separately for dynamic admission. Bay geometry has a bounded 48-entry cache and typed attributes. Folded roof emission is staged, finite/size checked, outward oriented and bounded before append.

Baked model placements choose batching only when at least two instances save at least 1,024 referenced vertices. Lamp candidates have distinct custom/mast/pit caps; over-cap custom lamps prioritize aimed/always/corner-adjacent records, then remove nearest neighbours to preserve distribution. Per-frame lighting culls are a downstream responsibility.
