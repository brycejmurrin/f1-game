## Archived code and diagnostic limits

S18 uses CDP heap usage after three garbage collections, with boot/build/settled stages and equal wall-time soaking. Backend binding is read from diagnostics rather than the user's storage preference; fallback invalidates a GLX/TLX comparison. Frame waits have deadlines, TLX memory counters are flat fields, and absent WeakMap size is unavailable evidence rather than zero retained GPU state. Although its comments demand early backend assertion, the actual final mismatch verdict occurs after both legs have completed. This is historical harness source, not a new benchmark result.

S19 measures software readback with fixed scale and separate OFF/gather/tap legs, setting storage before scripts. It records soft path, present size, upscale state and wall/await timing. Its backend field still reads an info property that S18 explains is unavailable, frame progression has unbounded animation waits, and warm/soft-present failures are swallowed. Those limitations prevent treating a neat summary as proof of successful WGX binding or reliable player FPS.

S20 observes one canvas pixel, adapter identity and GPU errors across flag stacks. S21 provides stronger controlled evidence for vertex_index: an offscreen target avoids canvas-readback interference; stride and three attributes stay fixed; queue writes avoid mapped-allocation confounds; arithmetic versus storage encoding separates causes; whole, split, offset and indexed draws cross the boundary. Alpha distinguishes missing rasterization from a zero index. Adapter fallback is marked as non-independent. Its header mentions a no-index storage control that the implemented pipeline list does not include, another reason to distinguish commentary from actual case construction.

S22's 18-case HUD matrix captures geometry, visibility, classes, settings and optional accessibility output; successful capture does not assert layout correctness. S23's 160-cell readability sweep measures visual font size with CSS zoom, target size, truncation and non-scrollable clipping, then deduplicates and caps samples. Its minimal JSON-RPC client only matches responses to requests and assumes fenced JSON output; it is not a complete general MCP client.

Relocation further limits rerunning these programs: S20/S21 retain relative imports whose library targets are absent at their archived locations; S18/S19 and S23 retain ancestor-based roots that no longer resolve the repository root. ## Track evidence and renderer review

S24 must be read with its correction banner: its original one-to-one Monaco index mapping, nearest-vertex snapping and supposedly missing derivation are contradicted by later measurements. It directs readers to START-LINES.md for nearest-segment derivation, reversed Monaco storage, independent source-line estimation and a scenery-transform blocker. A start-fraction change rotates racing space and can move landmarks into the road, so derivation is distinct from safe application. The old handoff's per-corner direction estimates failed calibration; start and finish lines can differ; uncertain coordinates are explicitly not guesses to apply.

S25 records a campaign that completed all 40 then-current circuits, with many classics already complete. It explains why build success missed clipping and coplanar regressions: arc compression, authored planting, internal shared-emitter overlaps and repeated level water sheets. indexSolid reserves ground before later scatter but cannot remove planting already authored. The ledger documents Singapore's direction reversal, new field-copy and transform coverage gaps, and why imported geometry cannot independently validate its own real racing direction. Suzuka's figure-eight and classic Kyalami's era are explicit exceptions to naive direction classification. Its status counts and geometry totals are dated results, not present inventories.

S26's assigned opening argues that the renderer boundary already existed: swappability and async boot do not by themselves reduce large shader/state maintenance cost. Shared shader chunks and measured draw-call bottlenecks matter more than a speculative backend port; instancing is valuable only where measurement supports it. The later header records the formerly future WebGPU work as implemented, while even that header's default-backend statement is older than current AGENTS.md. The source's remaining design sections were not assigned here, so this map does not claim to have read or validate their details.
