## Concept measurement and camera fitting

The measurement tail exports geometry dimensions and tables for shipped/new body IDs, sampling nose cross sections, sidepod shoulders and engine-cover sections in millimetres. Team selection, an ID environment filter and JSON output make it a comparison CLI; imports and setup are outside this batch.

The overlay utility consumes the geometry probe's indexed positions and part ranges, projects them through solved front/rear cameras, rasterizes a z-buffer of part IDs, and overlays colored boundaries on a reference concept sheet through Sharp. Its caller chooses the output PNG. Camera constants, reference offsets and clipping are specific to that sheet. The optimizer accepts weighted 3D-to-sheet landmarks as JSON, optionally fixes the principal point, and runs a constrained multi-start Nelder-Mead fit. Its JSON includes residuals and a look target on the solved ray. The small named measurement fixture is comparison data. These utilities support design investigation; their projections and sampled envelopes do not certify every mesh surface.
