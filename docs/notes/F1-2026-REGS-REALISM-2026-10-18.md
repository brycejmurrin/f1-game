## 2026 calendar

The December 2025 calendar (formula1.com) listed 24 rounds. Bahrain and Saudi Arabia were cancelled in April 2026, and the Bahrain GP moved to Sepang (2–4 Oct). The game's `REAL_2026` preset already encodes the 23-round calendar with dates and sprints. This research used the pre-cancellation calendar, so `REAL_2026` is the reference.
