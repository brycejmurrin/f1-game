# Crew animation: Blender clips for the garage and the pit stop (2026-10-24)

A·W49, from Bryce (02:18Z): "install blender and use tools to add more options to
the crew garage leave and enter animations and add same models and animations to
when in game we pull into pit lane and have tires swapped".

## Pipeline

- Blender 4.2.23 LTS, official tarball
  (https://download.blender.org/release/Blender4.2/blender-4.2.23-linux-x64.tar.xz),
  headless: `blender -b --python tools/gen/blender/crew-choreo.py -- --output-dir artifacts/crew-choreo --write-runtime [--render]`.
- The script authors every crew member as an Empty with keyframed location,
  Z rotation and custom properties (`crouch lean handY reach wave hold`), all
  SINE / EASE_IN_OUT. A car proxy is animated from a Python port of
  `js/garage/arrival.js`'s path and timing. The `.blend` lands in
  `artifacts/crew-choreo/` (not committed); its keys are regenerated from the script.
- Export: each channel as its own keys (`[t0,v0,t1,v1,…]`), holds dropped.
  The runtime (`GarageCrewChoreo.value`) applies the same ease, so no dense
  samples ship (18 KB for 7 clips and 35 members). `walk`, the gait phase
  `GarageCrewMotion` replays, is each body's travel along its own facing,
  integrated at 60 Hz from Blender's evaluated world matrices. The PUSH-OUT crew
  rides the car until its release key: those members are baked into linear
  keys at 12 Hz.
- The script asserts that every hand target is within the two-bone arm span
  (it stops at 0.66 m; today's maximum is 0.613 m). The source SHA256 is
  stamped in the runtime block, and `garage-crew-choreo.test.mjs` fails if
  it is stale.

## Why rigid articulated bodies, not a skinned glTF

- The model pack (`assets/pack/models`, AX26 `.bin`) is static meshes only.
  `js/render/shared/gltf.js` has no skin, bone or animation code on GLX, TLX or
  WGX. A skinned crew would need a new skinning path on three backends.
- `GarageCrewMotion` already draws a Blender-driven articulated body (12 rigid
  parts in livery colours, built once). It needs no asynchronous asset and
  has nothing to degrade when the pack is missing. Every new clip reuses that
  body, so the garage crew and the pit crew are literally the same models.

## Garage variants

| id | way | extra s | members | what happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| marshal | in | 1.6 | 3 | one walks into the lane and calls the car back; two meet it at the wing and the rear tyre |
| inspect | in | 4.2 | 5 | four walk in from the side walls and crouch over each tyre; an engineer brings a tablet |
| debrief | in | 3.4 | 4 | engineer and mechanic lean into either side of the cockpit; one checks the nose; claps |
| sendoff | out | 0.6 | 6 | six line the doorway, arms up as it rolls, then clap |
| pushout | out | 0 | 3 | two push off the rear wing, let go as it picks up, wave; one points down the lane |
| check | out | 3.0 | 4 | front tyre, rear tyre, wing and cockpit checks, all step back, thumbs up |

MIX, the new default for both ENTER and LEAVE, rotates through
`current`, the three variants and `service`. It never repeats on consecutive
visits, and the count persists (`apex26.crewVisit`). It resolves once per
playback object (`GarageArrival.crewFor`, a WeakMap), so `pose`, `poseOut`,
`duration` and `finish` agree with no change to `setup-camera.js`. A test checks
every member against the car's swept footprint at 20 Hz, for both lane sides
and all three angles.

## The in-race stop

- `js/garage/pit-crew.js` (`PitCrewAnim`) plays the `pit` clip at
  `u * 2.2 s`, where u is `PitLane.stopAnim(c).u`. The clip has no clock of its
  own, so it cannot move the stop's timing, physics or determinism. Steps match
  stopAnim's own boundaries: jack < .15, off < .27, swap < .68, on < .80,
  check < .88, then drop. The tyre men's hands follow the wheel slide within
  8 cm. The jack saddles rise by `stopAnim.lift`.
- Hook: `js/car/car-draw.js drawPitCrew` asks `PitCrewAnim.draw` first. Any
  false answer leaves the static `CarMesh.getCrewMesh` crew, which is one draw.
  `pit-lane.js` is unchanged.
- **Player only, within 60 m.** AI stops keep the static mesh. The animated
  crew is ~130 draws (10 members × 12 parts plus guns and jacks), so one stopped
  rival would cost as much as several cars.
