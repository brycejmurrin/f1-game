## Method (so the next owner can repeat it)

Every row is a rect read from the live page after driving the real control, never a label or a comment.

- Sliders: set `input.value`, dispatch the slider's own `input` event, call `GameHud.invalidateFit()`, wait two settle
  periods, read `getBoundingClientRect()` of every `HudLayout.ELEMENTS` selector and every `.touchbtn`. Swept up, then
  down, to catch hysteresis. `__apex.headless(true)` after the race starts (as `tools/shot/hud-survey.mjs` does): with
  the 3D loop on, software GL starves the box and a sweep takes 10 minutes instead of 2.
- Slider direction: real `touchscreen.tap` at 10 / 25 / 75 / 90 % of the track, reading the value.
- ARRANGE HUD: tap PAUSE › SETTINGS › HUD & CAMERA › ARRANGE HUD, pick each widget in `#hud-arrange-element`, click
  `+ + − − − +`, drag by (±40, ±25) px with CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`, push the widget 130 vw off screen and
  click BRING INTO VIEW, click RESET, set VISIBILITY off then back.
- Persistence: write through the slider, reload, start the race again, compare rects.
- The probe scripts lived in the session's ignored `scratch/control-audit/` and are not in the repo.

## Other position controls (read, not yet driven)

- REPOSITION TOUCH CONTROLS (`js/ui/dock-layout.js`): offsets are clamped to `[0, 0.35]` of the usable pad, inboard
  and up only. A drag outward or down from home is dead by design. Its SIZE − / + drive `pm-btnscale`.
- MOVE & SIZE number fields (`pm-hl-x`, `pm-hl-y`, `pm-hl-s`): show the raw stored values, so SIZE shows the same
  `63.76` as the ARRANGE readout. UP / DOWN: positive moves down.
