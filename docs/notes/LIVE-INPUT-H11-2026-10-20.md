# Live input latency and main-thread jank on the phone profile (B·H11, 2026-10-20)

Lane: Fleet B perf hunter B·H11. The ask (Bryce, 2026-10-20): the live site "feels a little laggy" on a phone
(844×390 landscape, touch). This note ranks the main-thread lag sources by measured ms and records the fix.

## Method

All runs: this container's Chromium, SwiftShader, context 844×390 at DPR 2 with `isMobile` and `hasTouch`,
`steerMode` touch and AUTO throttle, Monza, `race()` + `go()`. CPU ×4 (`Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate`) is the
phone stand-in. `__apex.headless(true)` skips `render()`: on SwiftShader the draw costs ~12 s a frame and drowns
everything else, and the renderer is B·H10's lane. What remains is input, physics, AI, audio, HUD, style and
layout: the main-thread work a phone pays on top of its GPU.

Scripts (scratch, not committed): `scratch/h11/probe.mjs` (spans, LoAF, event timing, trace with
invalidation tracking, real CDP touch drags), `cpu.mjs` (V8 sampling profile), `episodes.mjs` (slowest
`updateHud` calls with child breakdown), `fitlog.mjs` (every full HUD fit and the key part that changed;
`hud.js` served instrumented through `page.route`), `style-ab.mjs` (CSSOM rule deletion A/B, trace-measured
`UpdateLayoutTree` per wall-second and per frame).

## Ranked lag sources (CPU ×4, ms per frame unless stated)

| # | source | measured | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Style recalc driven by one root `:has()` rule. Every gap-chip text write re-runs `:has()` invalidation on `<html>`/`<body>` and restyles ~425 elements, about 1.4 times a frame | 20.4–20.9 ms/f base; 2.3–7.3 with the rule deleted; 6.2–6.6 with all 106 `:has()` rules deleted | `css/hud.css:222` `.hud-gaps:not(:has(> div:not(:empty)))`, fed by `hText` at `js/ui/hud.js:2279-2280` |
| 2 | HUD 10 Hz tick forced layouts: tick frames p50 4.7, p90 43, max 234 ms | full fit 30–116 ms (start of race; ×1 steady state: one every ~3 s); announce lane `laneRect` 10–39 ms; `phoneFitStampSync` 50–60 ms | `js/ui/hud.js` `fitHud` :1014, `announceLane` :620, `phoneFitStampSync` :937 |
| 3 | Physics + AI catch-up (fixed 1/60 s steps, ≤ 5 a frame) | `update` 42.8 ms/f, of which `updateCar` 32.6 | `js/game.js:4347`, `:4696` (physics lane, not changed here) |
| 4 | `hud-layout` readout clearance re-run from a ResizeObserver and the pack follow-ups | `clearReadouts` 3.2 ms/f across callers | `js/ui/hud-layout.js:764`, observer :883 |
| 5 | Input read path: `remoteBrake`/`remoteThrottle`/`lookingBack` ask `UiLayers.navOpen()` before the cheap `remoteActive()` test, every physics step, with no phone paired | 2.2 ms/f inclusive | `js/input/input.js:1756-1757`, `:1864` |

Not lag sources, measured or read:
- Touch handlers: `onTouchStart` 6–13 ms once at touch-down at ×4, moves well under 1 ms. The window touch
  listeners are passive, the canvas ones must `preventDefault`, and coalesced pointer samples are already read.
- Touch input reaches physics in the same frame: Chrome aligns `touchmove` to rAF, and `Input.steer(stepDt)`
  reads the latest finger position at the next physics step. Render interpolation shows the pose up to one
  1/60 s step behind (`renderAlpha`). That delay is part of the fixed-step design.
- Default BUTTONS steering ramps to full lock in 250 ms (`KEY_RAMP_IN` 4/s). That is a feel setting (STEER
  RATE / SPEED STEER sliders), not latency, and it is left alone.
