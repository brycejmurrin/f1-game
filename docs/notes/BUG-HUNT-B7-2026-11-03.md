## The guard

`tests/unit/menu-quit-tail.test.mjs` now parses the `MenuQuitTail.wire({…})` call in game.js and fails when a
shorthand field names a `let` that game.js reassigns: such a field is a boot-time snapshot. It failed on the deploy
tip with `qualiNet, netLobby, tyres, raceTyreWear`. `qualiNet` and `tyres` are assigned once before the wire, so they
were harmless, but they are getters now so the rule has no exceptions.
