## Correction (after the reverts, 2026-11-06 ~09:30Z)

The baseline `a0d2161e` predates #4865 (10:15 PT) and #4906 (20:46 PT) as well as #5127, so the first table could
not tell which of them moved the cluster. Re-measured per merge, title only, same cell and export, against the
re-blessed golden (x 506.5): `e2432a0c` (the merge before #4865) = **472**; `a208bfcc` (#4865) = 506.5; `ef5b24bc`
(#4906) = 506.5; `4b1d2f02` (the tip after #5142 reverted #5127 + #5138, and #5143 reverted #4982 + #4897) = 506.5.
So **#4865 moved the title cluster**, the three later title PRs kept its position, and the #5142 revert left the
cluster where #4865 put it. The appearance row did go away with #5143 (the studio top 258.5 → 128.5, the baseline
value).

No global regression reproduces: nothing in the Nov 4–5 merges touches the root font, UI scale, overlay padding,
safe-area or orientation handling, and 17 of 20 screens are pixel-identical to the Nov 4 baseline with his export.
What the phone can still show that Chromium cannot:

1. **A stale or mixed shell.** `docs/notes/TITLE-CLUSTER-SW-2026-11-06.md` §Lead A: a Home-Screen launch on a slow
   link can boot the previous shell with the previous CSS for a whole session, so four title fixes "did nothing" in a
   row. #5138 (merged after the report) auto-applies a newer shell when the player is idle. The first launch after it
   lands is the one that proves it.
2. **WebKit's handling of the cluster's `width` inside a zoomed box** (same note): Chromium honours it at every
   geometry; the device did not. #5138's length-only margins are the attempt; not measurable here.
3. **Per-orientation sizes (SEPARATE by default, #4897).** If the app was ever in portrait after Nov 4 13:56 PT, HUD
   SIZE / BUTTON SIZE / looks changed since then belong to that orientation only. Not a bug; a surprise if unnoticed.

## The pin

`tests/specs/phone-layout-pin.spec.js` boots Bryce's cell exactly as above (852×393, `display-mode: standalone`,
59/21/59, touch, his export) and holds 12 screens' structural boxes and the global tokens to
`tests/data/phone-layout-pin.json` at 1 px. It would have failed on #5127 (`#menu-buttons` +34 px) and on #4897
(`#appearance-studio` top +130 px): both wanted, both then re-blessed on purpose with
`node tools/ui/phone-layout-pin.mjs --update` and a sentence in the PR. The golden was first blessed at `f7761eee` (unchanged at `1485ef32`, the #5136 revert) and is re-blessed at `4b1d2f02` after #5142 and #5143: the only values that moved are the DISPLAY HUD sample (now pinned by size, CI puts the panel's GPU rows elsewhere) and the appearance studio's top (258.5 → 128.5); the title cluster stayed at 506.5. 12 screens pinned, twice in a row, with `navigator.standalone` and `display-mode: standalone` emulated (the install banner stays hidden, as on the Home-Screen app).
