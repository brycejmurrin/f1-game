# Load hunt — boot, race entry, caching (A·H15, 2026-10-24)

Lane A·H15 (Fleet A, wave 34): boot, menu→race entry, track switches, repeat visits. Prior art read first
(DEVICE-FARM-ANALYSIS-2026-10-24, PERF-KNOWN-WINS-2026-10-23, BOOT-LOAD-TOOLING-RESEARCH-2026-10-21,
PERF-HUNT-BOOT-2026-10-17, DEFECT-LEDGER); nothing below repeats a landed or refuted row. Software numbers
(SwiftShader / Dawn-SwiftShader in this container) rank work; they are not device evidence.

## Method

- `tools/perf/race-entry-timer.mjs` (GLX pin, Monza day) with a CDP CPU profile, summarised by
  `tools/perf/cpuprofile-summary.mjs`.
- A deploy-shaped site: `node tools/desktop/stage.mjs --out artifacts/site --stamp --at 21999` (the
  pages.yml staging: minified, `?v=<content hash>` tags), served on a non-dev hostname
  (`apex.test`, mapped to 127.0.0.1 with `--host-resolver-rules`, made a secure context with
  `--unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure`) so `sw.js` runs its DEPLOYED cache-first branch, not the
  `DEV_HOST` network-first one. CDP network throttle 150 ms RTT, 1.5 MB/s down. Script:
  `scratch/load/sw-overhead.mjs` (not committed; scratch).
- Boot timeline from an init script that wraps `navigator.gpu.requestAdapter` / `requestDevice` and
  listens for `DOMContentLoaded` and `apex-gfx-live`.

## L1 measurement

Warm SW reloads of two deploy-shaped stagings served side by side (before = 49766d7a, after = this fix),
arms alternating, 2 rounds × 3 reloads per arm and pin, 150 ms RTT, SwiftShader WebGPU. Medians, ms from
navigation start (`scratch/load/ab-summary.mjs` over `artifacts/logs/ab-early-adapter.log`):

| pin | arm | n | DCL | adapter #1 sent | adapter #1 answered | three's adapter sent | gfx-live |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| unset | before | 6 | 890 | 791 | 1355 | 1463 | 3426 |
| unset | after | 6 | 797 | 381 | 1320 | 1385 | 2652 |
| three | before | 6 | 829 | 1008 | 1710 | 1711 | 3170 |
| three | after | 6 | 894 | 353 | 1298 | 1422 | 2929 |

Reading: the request now leaves ~400–650 ms earlier on both pins. On the unset default the ANSWER moves
only 35 ms: the adapter promise resolves at a main-thread task boundary, and the main thread is still
evaluating the deferred scripts, so the win there is small. On an explicit THREE pick (Bryce's iPhone
setting) TLX's own sniff request is gone: the adapter is answered 412 ms sooner, three's own request goes
out 289 ms sooner, and the bind lands 241 ms sooner. `gfx-live` spread inside one arm is 2.2–4.4 s, so treat
the end-to-end number as direction only, and the chain numbers as the evidence. Device proof (WebKit's
requestAdapter latency on the iPhone) is not measured; this is a software ranking.
