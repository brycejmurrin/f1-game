# 2026 grid liveries — design language reference (2026-10-18)

Research pass for the livery-design worker (LD): what each of the eleven 2026
launch liveries does with colour, which PARTS carry it, the recurring motifs,
and where marks and numbers sit. It feeds three slices: more paintable parts,
new pattern designs, and logo/number placement. Companion to
`LIVERY-2026-REFERENCE.md` (which settles the per-team cover/spine colours that
ship); this note is about the design VOCABULARY, not the shipped team values.

**Originality rule.** Apex 26 is an unofficial fan game. Everything built from
this note is a MOTIF (a split, a fade, a chevron run, a keyline), never a copy
of a team's or sponsor's livery, wordmark, crest or trademark. No real sponsor
name, logo shape or team crest is drawn; the "logo" options are generic,
original crests and the placement slots they use.

## Motifs that recur (the design vocabulary)

1. **Split blocks** — left/right (Cadillac), upper/lower (Aston green over
   black, Mercedes silver over black), front/rear. Already in the game:
   `bodySplit: "lr"`, `twoTone`. Missing: an upper/lower split with a hard
   or keylined edge.
2. **Gradient fades** — Mercedes silver→black front-to-rear; Cadillac's
   halftone fade between halves. In the game: `gradient` (smooth),
   `pixelFade`. Missing: a HALFTONE (dot-size) fade, and a fade made of
   repeating chevrons.
3. **Keylines / outlines** — Aston lime line, Haas red trim, Williams red+white
   keyline, Red Bull's white outline around marks. In the game: `pinstripes`.
   Missing: a single bold keyline that FOLLOWS a split edge (two-colour
   block + thin contrasting line on the boundary).
4. **Parallel stripe triples** — Mercedes' three stripes over the sidepod
   fading toward the cover. In the game: `ribbon` (one band), `pinstripes`
   (thin). Missing: a triple BAND set, raked, that tapers or fades out.
5. **Chevrons** — Cadillac's chevron-built fade; Haas's diagonal slash on the
   nose. In the game: `brokenChevron`. Missing: a solid stacked chevron
   (arrowheads nested toward the nose), and a single big diagonal SLASH.
6. **Texture inside a block** — Red Bull's pixel etch in the blue. In the
   game: `pixelFade`, `honeycomb`. Covered.
7. **Colour REACHING the extremities** — every 2026 car paints the NOSE TIP,
   the WING ENDPLATES and the HALO deliberately (Alpine's dark-blue tip,
   Red Bull red tip, McLaren papaya endplates, Cadillac chrome halo, Ferrari
   red halo, Mercedes black halo). 2026 rules pushed teams to paint more and
   leave less bare carbon (TA, McLaren). This is the PAINTABLE-PARTS gap.
8. **Marks on fixed stations** — every car uses the same small set of mark
   STATIONS: nose top/tip, sidepod flank, cover flank, cover crown, fin,
   rear-wing endplate outer face, front-wing endplate, halo top. Numbers: nose
   (all eleven), fin or cover flank (most), sometimes the RW-EP.

## What this means for the build (feeds the three slices)

- **Zones (parts)**: the endplates (front and rear), the nose tip, the fin and
  the mirror housings are where real 2026 cars put contrast colour. A zone
  colour must reach them; parts that are fixed carbon/black keep their own
  material (issue #2796 items 1–3: a FINISH must also reach halo, sidepod panel
  and cover-stripe parts painted in body colour).
- **Patterns**: add the missing motifs above as ORIGINAL procedural art —
  upper/lower split with keyline, halftone fade, stacked solid chevrons,
  raked triple bands, single diagonal slash. Each is generic geometry, owned by
  no team.
- **Logos and numbers**: offer original crests (shield, roundel, wing,
  arrowhead — no real mark) and the eight stations in motif 8 as placement
  choices, with a contrast outline option (the "white outline round the mark"
  idea), so a mark stays legible on any base.
