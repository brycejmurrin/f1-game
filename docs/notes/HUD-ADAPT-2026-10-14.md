# Race HUD sizing and adaptability pass (2026-10-14)

Owner report: "lots of sizing issues, adaptability with buttons and all the elements" on the
race HUD (phone, landscape). Branch `cursor/hud-adapt-3f9a`, from ship `f5381b40e`.

Method: two read-only Opus reviews of `css/hud.css` / `css/touch-controls.css` / `js/ui/hud.js`
(no browser), plus `node tools/shot/hud-mock.mjs` over 168 cells — 7 devices (667x375, 740x360,
844x390 both notch shapes, 932x430, 1180x820 touch, 1280x720 mouse) x cockpit/chase x HUD SIZE
70/100/150 x BUTTON SIZE 100/200 x shipped / every opt-in on — shot before and after, same cells.
Reports under `artifacts/hud-mock/adapt-baseline` and `adapt-after3` (this container only).

The 150% trade is deliberate: those cells used to be "clean" because the dock cap shrank BOOST to
50px and the pedals to 50px; with full-size thumbs the radio card's lane is narrower and the card
(now clamped to three lines) reaches the gearbox on a 360px phone. The structural answer is the
allocator in `HUD-PHONE-LAYOUT-PLAN-2026-10-10.md`.
