race HUD (phone, landscape). Branch `cursor/hud-adapt-3f9a`, from ship `f5381b40e`.

## Round 2 (same day): visual review + logic bug hunt

Two more Opus reviews on the batch-1 tree: one looked at twelve of the mock shots as a HUD
designer, one hunted readout-logic bugs with `vm` repros. Fixes landed, each pinned where a
unit file could see it:

| finding | fix |
|---|---|
| The caution flag and the radio card stepped 74px under the tower whenever the gap chip was dropped — the literal encoded "below the sector plate", which only matters when a wide dock inset pushes the plate into the flag's columns | 44px (clears a two-line dropped strip); fitHud sets `data-flag-low` when the plate's left edge reaches the flag, and only then the flag takes the plate's measured bottom |
| The pending DELTA slot (`visibility: hidden`) kept ~95px of top-band width on phones, capping the band at ~0.95 and dropping the gap chip; on desktop it left the four live boxes 56px off the flag's axis | touch: the pending plate leaves the layout (`display: none`) and the paint asks for one refit when the number lands; desktop: the labelled plate stays with only its value invisible |
| Gap chip at 11.4px painted beside a 128px map | 14px base, 3px 8px padding (floor unchanged) |
| OT / AERO idle chips drew the CHASE/PAUSE button outline and read as disabled buttons | a left stripe in the chip's own hue (`--ot-hue` / `--aero-hue`, declared once) |
| Cluster edges ragged (centred max-content chips), ERS label tracked "E R S", tyre chip the only unframed readout, sector plate 1.6 leading, desktop row with four vertical centres, tower labels 9.8px at HUD 70% | chips stretch their column, SPEED sits on the ERS bar's left edge, 0.06em tracking, a plate on TYRES, 1.4 leading, `align-items: center` on the desktop row, an 11px painted floor on the labels |
| RELATIVE rows `fitRows` dropped to clear BRAKE came back on the next tick (fit only re-ran every 10th tick): a half-row over BRAKE flickering at 1 Hz | a per-row drop mask the tick respects; pinned in hud-relative.test ("tick N: the rows fitRows dropped stay dropped") |
| OVERTAKE chip chose its class before the gate: a held lap-0 grant or a grant under a safety car pulsed the yellow "ready" plate while the text read OT · CAUTION | class decided after `otOff`, like the text; pinned in hud-feel.test |

| Sector split signed before rounding (`★-0.000`), DELTA colour from the unrounded value, splits ≥ 60 s as `75.123`, `▲ VER --s` on a non-finite gap, `~0L` on a set with under half a lap left, a damage label stale when a non-worst part moved, REL screen-reader rows "2 lap up" / "-- seconds", `aria-label` on role-less OT / AERO / BB divs | `signedFixed` everywhere, m:ss past a minute, no "s" without a number, `<1L` / "under a lap left", every part's tenth in the damage signature, plural and "gap unknown" wording, `role="img"` on the three chips |

Same 168 mock cells after round 2: 22 red cells (18 after round 1, 86 at the baseline); the
radio card's gearbox / speed overlaps went from 17 cells to 2. The remaining reds are HUD 150%
and BUTTON 200% stress cells (TYRES vs RELATIVE / STRATEGY above a full-size dock) and the
desktop cockpit TYRES × BB.

Named and not done this round: `RADIO_TOP_MIN` 96 → 200 (an iPad's 119px slot wraps the card to
four lines) — hud-feel pins 134px as a slot and the fit's idempotence rides on the slot decision,
so it needs a per-width floor; the tyre temperature band (±0.55 × window) vs the physics window
(±1.0) and the undercut cue's 20 s window are design calls, not bugs, left as they are; REL's
`fitKey` reads `innerWidth` per tick (a forced layout on Android); the TT ghost chip colour from
the unrounded delta; `skinAccent` never checks 4.5:1.
