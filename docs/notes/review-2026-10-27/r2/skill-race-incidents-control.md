The routing (description → workflow.md "SC never deploys") is right, and steps 1-2 (caution enabled, debris active) are correct and cheap. Step 3 (workflow.md:130) is where it goes wrong. The agent reproduces the pile-up, then reads `caution({hazards:true})` once after the cars stop. It sees total < 10, or 0, and step 3's last sentence lets it conclude "too few settled hazards → VSC/yellow, by design". The skill never says that:
(a) the piled-up cars themselves are not hazards, since only debris shard slots, displaced cones and broken panels count;
(b) a shard counts only while asleep, on the surface and within ±1.6 m of road height, and it despawns 4 s after it sleeps;
(c) the shard pool is capped (16 on the mobile tier Bryce plays) and recycles the oldest.
So the pile-up that "should" bring the SC is one whose shards never sleep at the same moment. The real question is whether a stranded-car pile-up should count as a hazard, which is a design gap in hazards(). A threshold tweak does not answer it, and the skill gives the agent no way to see this.
