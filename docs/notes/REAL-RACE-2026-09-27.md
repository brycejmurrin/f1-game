# Real race emulation — design note (2026-09-27)

The Data Hub's **RACE IT** tab turns one real Grand Prix's timing into a race
the player can join: the real grid, every AI car steered lap by lap to its real
pace and gaps, its real stops and compounds, its retirement lap, and the race's
safety-car windows, with the player in one real driver's seat. First target:
the 2026 Azerbaijan GP (OpenF1 session 11377, run 2026-09-26).

## Data (OpenF1, `https://api.openf1.org/v1`)

Ten bodies per race, all through `F1API.request` (the queue, the 28/min
window, the retry ladder). Measured for Baku 2026:

| endpoint | rows | bytes | used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/sessions?session_key=` | 1 | 0.4 KB | date, `gmt_offset` (time of day), circuit, country |
| `/drivers` | 22 | 9 KB | number, code, name, team |
| `/laps` | 983 | 487 KB | per-lap durations (never cached by api.js: over its 256 KB cap) |
| `/stints` | 58 | 8 KB | compounds, stint lap ranges |
| `/pit` | 36 | 6 KB | in-laps |
| `/race_control` | 141 | 36 KB | SAFETY CAR DEPLOYED / IN THIS LAP, VSC, RED |
| `/weather` | 168 | 36 KB | rainfall → dry / wet / rain |
| `/session_result` | 22 | 4 KB | classification, laps completed, DNF, DSQ |
| `/overtakes` | 160 | 20 KB | every pass: who, whom, when, for which position (third pass) |
| `/position` | 311 | 36 KB | the FIRST snapshot per driver = the grid (`/starting_grid` is a 404) |

CORS: `access-control-allow-origin: *` (checked with curl on 2026-09-27; the
hub's LIVE and TELEMETRY tabs already fetch it from the browser). Licence:
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — non-commercial with attribution; the tab credits it.
Jolpica has no race control, weather or grid snapshot, so it is not used here.

The compact script (~20 KB) is cached under `apex26.realrace.v1.<sessionKey>`;
the raw bodies are not. `tests/fixtures/openf1-baku-2026-race.json` is the
real session trimmed to the fields the builder reads (115 KB).

## The script

```
{ v, source, sessionKey, meetingKey, year, name, session, circuit, country,
  trackId, dateStart, tod, weather, laps,
  drivers: [{ num, code, name, team, teamId, grid, pos, lapsDone, dnf,
              laps: [s | null …], stints: [{c, from, to}], pits: [lap …] }],
  cautions: [{ level: 2|3|4, from, to, cause }] }
```

`trackIdFor` resolves the circuit by name first (Italy has Monza and Imola),
then the country's current-season circuit. `todFor` reads the local start hour
off `date_start + gmt_offset`. Every code maps onto a roster seat
(`RealRace.mapField`: by driver code, then a free seat of the same team); a
seat with no real driver reads as did-not-start.

## The director (`js/race/real-race.js`)

Nothing in `js/game.js` changed shape: four hook lines (create, `update(dt)`
before race control, `stop()` in `quitToMenu`, two `G` members) and a
`hold(level, cause)` in `js/race/race-control.js`.

1. **Stage** (`RealRace.launch`): the DailyChallenge pattern — set `G.flow`,
   `trackIdx`, `teamIdx`/`driverIdx` (the seat), `raceLaps`, weather, time of
   day, wear ON; `G.startRace()`. `stop()` restores every setting it touched.
2. **Arm** (first countdown frame): `G.gridUp(realOrder)` — one re-grid, no RNG
   draw — then over each car: the pit plan cut from the real stints
   (`planFor`: in-laps mapped onto the sim distance, never two in a lap, never
   the last), the real start compound fitted, `dnfAt = (lapsDone + 0.5) / laps`
   for the retirements (`null` for a finisher: no random failures), and ONE
   base pace for the field so the differences are the data's.
3. **Pace loop** (each AI line crossing): `c.skill = base × mul`, where
   `mul = clamp((1 / rel) × (1 + KP·err / lapS), 0.90, 1.10)`.
   `rel` is the real lap relative to the field median that lap (pit and
   caution laps neutral); `err` is the sim gap to the REFERENCE car minus the
   scaled real gap, in seconds, positive when the car has fallen behind its
   real gap. The reference car is the real leader at that lap among the AI
   seats; its own crossings pin `K` (sim seconds per real second — this box
   runs ~1.5× real), so a condensed race compresses the gaps in proportion.
   `KP = 0.6` recovers six tenths of a drift per lap. The player is never
   touched (AGENTS.md: the arc must not reach the driver — this touches AI
   skill only).
4. **Flags**: while the leader's lap is inside a window, `G.holdCaution(level)`;
   race control's `hold` keeps the level at least that high, exempt from the
   90 s SC cap and the MIN_HOLD drop, and hands back to the hazard loop on
   release. A red flag fires once through `applyCaution` (its own procedure).

Distance: FULL, or 1/2, 1/5, 1/10 of the real laps; every stop, flag and
retirement maps proportionally (`simLapFor` / `realLapFor`).

## Jump in at any lap (2026-09-27, second pass)

The RACE IT tab's **JUMP IN AT** picker names a real lap (the eventful ones
are labelled: safety car, VSC, red flag, who went out, rain). `fieldAt(script,
L)` reconstructs the race at t0, the instant the real leader completed lap
L-1: for each driver the laps completed by t0 (from the cumulative lap
times), how far round the lap in progress the car was BY TIME (`(t0 -
cum[k]) / lap[k+1]`, so a slow car is placed where it was, not where a fast one
would be), the stint in progress with the set's age (laps run plus
`tyre_age_at_start`), the stops made so far, and `retired` when the car's data
ends before t0 (no time for the lap after its last completed one).

The director arms on a COUNTDOWN frame (its hook now runs at the top of
`game.js` `update()` in every state — the first version was only called in the
race state, so the re-grid landed one frame after lights-out) and, on the first
GREEN frame, drops the field in: `c.lap`, `c.s`, `c.prog`, the world pose and
render anchors as `__apex.aiPlace()` writes them, a cruise speed
(`JUMP_SPEED` of `vTop()`), the set fitted with its wear (`age / planLaps` in sim
laps, capped below the cliff) and `pitStops` set so the pit plan's next stop is
the right one; cars already out are parked at the wall the way `retireCar()`
parks one, without the broadcast. The race clock and every car's `totalT` are
seeded to `K0 · t0`, with `K0 = referencePole() / best clean real lap` (the
model's quali lap over the race's fastest lap, a few per cent low, which the
reference car's next crossing corrects), and the reference car's earlier
crossings are seeded the same way so the closed loop has a gap to measure from
the first crossing.

Fixes from the review of the first pass: ONE reference car for the whole race
(the AI seat whose real data runs longest, best classified first) instead of a
per-lap leader; the correction gain is per SIM lap at a condensed distance
(`K · ref · realLaps / simLaps`, where K carries the compression); a red-flag
lap rewind is followed, not re-measured; a driver with no roster seat is
refused rather than seated in someone else's car; cautions are cut once at
arm; an in-progress race (no winner, laps still coming in) builds and plays but
is never cached; a red flag applied with the CAUTIONS switch off now runs its
procedure to the restart (it used to hold the field at 2 % of top speed for
ever); `hold()` lowers and releases at once (the hazard loop never lowered a
held flag with debris off, and MIN_HOLD is for flicker, not lap windows). Rain
lap by lap (`rainByLap`: weather samples aligned to the leader's lap windows)
sets the start lap's weather and turns the sky with `startWeatherArc` when the
real rain started or stopped.

## The race lap by lap (2026-09-27, third pass)

The tab now shows the WHOLE race before the player picks a lap. `passesFor`
dates OpenF1's `/overtakes` rows onto laps through the overtaking driver's
`date_start` table (the overtaken driver's as the fallback), and drops a pass on
either side of a car retiring that lap (the feed logged 24 "passes" on Norris
and Gasly as the field streamed by their stopped cars); `lapBoard(script,
L)` orders the cars still running at lap L by their crossing time from the
director's cumulative table (`RealRace.cumTable`, filled through a car's
untimed laps with the reference lap so a car that went on is never dropped)
with the gap to the leader, the interval, in-lap / out-lap marks and the set,
then the cars already out, last out first; `raceBook(script)` folds the flags,
the rain, the passes, the stops, the retirements and the fastest lap into one
row per lap. The UI: **DRIVE AS** picks the seat once; **LAP BY LAP** lists the
51 rows with a JUMP IN on each (START on lap 1), and a row click opens that
lap's classification on demand; the **ENTRY LIST** keeps the per-driver JUMP IN
from the grid.

Fixes from the review of the second pass: a DSQ classified on the full
distance never retires (`dnfAtFor`); `fieldAt` reads a retirement off the
result's `lapsDone` as well as the timing (a car with a partial last lap);
`stop()` restores `trackIdx` and `flow`; a RESTART from the results puts the
start lap's weather back before the grid; the mid-race refit trims the tyre
log to ONE live stint and keeps `pitStops` = the plan's stops before the start
lap (so the next planned stop is the right one); the fuel lap is seeded; a
caution window is done once the field is past it (a red-flag lap rewind cannot
re-fly it); retirees are parked ON their lap; an unfinished race's raw bodies
are dropped from the API cache so the next visit refetches them.

## Known limits (v1)

- Real stints can outlast the sim compound's life (life is a fraction of the
  scheduled distance); the pit lane's "worn" rule then adds a stop.
- A mid-race drop-in places cars by time along the lap, not by their real
  x/y (`/location` is 40+ multi-MB requests per race); cars are spread ±1.5 m
  alternately so two on the same fraction do not spawn nose to tail.
- The rubber band still lifts AI behind the leading human; the loop absorbs
  it over the next laps rather than disabling it.
- No safety-car vehicle: a caution is a speed cap, as everywhere in the game.
- Seasons before 2026 map onto the 2026 roster by team where a code is
  unknown; drivers of a team the roster lacks are left out.
- OpenF1 free data lags a live session by ~30 min and locks out during it.
