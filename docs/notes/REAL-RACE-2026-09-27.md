# Real race emulation — design note (2026-09-27)

The Data Hub's **RACE IT** tab turns one real Grand Prix's timing into a race
the player can join: the real grid, every AI car steered lap by lap to its real
pace and gaps, its real stops and compounds, its retirement lap, and the race's
safety-car windows, with the player in one real driver's seat. First target:
the 2026 Azerbaijan GP (OpenF1 session 11377, run 2026-09-26).

## Data (OpenF1, `https://api.openf1.org/v1`)

Nine bodies per race, all through `F1API.request` (the queue, the 28/min
window, the retry ladder). Measured for Baku 2026:

| endpoint | rows | bytes | used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/sessions?session_key=` | 1 | 0.4 KB | date, `gmt_offset` (time of day), circuit, country |
| `/drivers` | 22 | 9 KB | number, code, name, team |
| `/laps` | 983 | 487 KB | per-lap durations (never cached by api.js: over its 256 KB cap) |
| `/stints` | 58 | 8 KB | compounds, stint lap ranges |
| `/pit` | 36 | 6 KB | in-laps |
| `/race_control` | 141 | 36 KB | SAFETY CAR DEPLOYED / IN THIS LAP, VSC, RED |
| `/weather` | 168 | 36 KB | rainfall → dry / wet / rain |
| `/session_result` | 22 | 4 KB | classification, laps completed, DNF |
| `/position` | 311 | 36 KB | the FIRST snapshot per driver = the grid (`/starting_grid` is a 404) |

CORS: `access-control-allow-origin: *` (checked with curl on 2026-09-27; the
hub's LIVE and TELEMETRY tabs already fetch it from the browser). Licence:
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — non-commercial with attribution; the tab credits it.
Jolpica has no race control, weather or grid snapshot, so it is not used here.

The compact script (~20 KB) is cached under `apex26.realrace.v1.<sessionKey>`;
the raw bodies are not. `tests/fixtures/openf1-baku-2026-race.json` is the
real session trimmed to the fields the builder reads (115 KB).

## The script

```
{ v, source, sessionKey, meetingKey, year, name, session, circuit, country,
  trackId, dateStart, tod, weather, laps,
  drivers: [{ num, code, name, team, teamId, grid, pos, lapsDone, dnf,
              laps: [s | null …], stints: [{c, from, to}], pits: [lap …] }],
  cautions: [{ level: 2|3|4, from, to, cause }] }
```

`trackIdFor` resolves the circuit by name first (Italy has Monza and Imola),
then the country's current-season circuit. `todFor` reads the local start hour
off `date_start + gmt_offset`. Every code maps onto a roster seat
(`RealRace.mapField`: by driver code, then a free seat of the same team); a
seat with no real driver reads as did-not-start.

## The director (`js/race/real-race.js`)

Nothing in `js/game.js` changed shape: four hook lines (create, `update(dt)`
before race control, `stop()` in `quitToMenu`, two `G` members) and a
`hold(level, cause)` in `js/race/race-control.js`.

1. **Stage** (`RealRace.launch`): the DailyChallenge pattern — set `G.flow`,
   `trackIdx`, `teamIdx`/`driverIdx` (the seat), `raceLaps`, weather, time of
   day, wear ON; `G.startRace()`. `stop()` restores every setting it touched.
2. **Arm** (first countdown frame): `G.gridUp(realOrder)` — one re-grid, no RNG
   draw — then over each car: the pit plan cut from the real stints
   (`planFor`: in-laps mapped onto the sim distance, never two in a lap, never
   the last), the real start compound fitted, `dnfAt = (lapsDone + 0.5) / laps`
   for the retirements (`null` for a finisher: no random failures), and ONE
   base pace for the field so the differences are the data's.
3. **Pace loop** (each AI line crossing): `c.skill = base × mul`, where
   `mul = clamp((1 / rel) × (1 + KP·err / lapS), 0.90, 1.10)`.
   `rel` is the real lap relative to the field median that lap (pit and
   caution laps neutral); `err` is the sim gap to the REFERENCE car minus the
   scaled real gap, in seconds, positive when the car has fallen behind its
   real gap. The reference car is the real leader at that lap among the AI
   seats; its own crossings pin `K` (sim seconds per real second — this box
   runs ~1.5× real), so a condensed race compresses the gaps in proportion.
   `KP = 0.6` recovers six tenths of a drift per lap. The player is never
   touched (AGENTS.md: the arc must not reach the driver — this touches AI
   skill only).
4. **Flags**: while the leader's lap is inside a window, `G.holdCaution(level)`;
   race control's `hold` keeps the level at least that high, exempt from the
   90 s SC cap and the MIN_HOLD drop, and hands back to the hazard loop on
   release. A red flag fires once through `applyCaution` (its own procedure).

Distance: FULL, or 1/2, 1/5, 1/10 of the real laps; every stop, flag and
retirement maps proportionally (`simLapFor` / `realLapFor`).

## Known limits (v1)

- Real stints can outlast the sim compound's life (life is a fraction of the
  scheduled distance); the pit lane's "worn" rule then adds a stop.
- The rubber band still lifts AI behind the leading human; the loop absorbs
  it over the next laps rather than disabling it.
- No safety-car vehicle: a caution is a speed cap, as everywhere in the game.
- Seasons before 2026 map onto the 2026 roster by team where a code is
  unknown; drivers of a team the roster lacks are left out.
- OpenF1 free data lags a live session by ~30 min and locks out during it.
