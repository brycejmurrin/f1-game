# Perf hunt 7: whole-tree code-survey plus the hunt-6 open list (2026-10-30)

PR #4211 is the follow-up to [PERF-HUNT-6-CPU-2026-10-30.md](PERF-HUNT-6-CPU-2026-10-30.md) (#4206). It also carries
Bryce's iPhone WebGL reports: "the full WebGL renderer shows a black screen" and "WebGL as a backup for three.js is
slow".

## Method
- The `code-survey` workflow (`focus: "perf"`, 3 rounds) ran 104 agents. Its `known` list held hunt 6's fixed rows,
  hunt 6's open rows and the prior-art rows. Result: 16 findings CONFIRMED by the verifier panel (15 perf, 1 bug), 0 dead code.
  Every physics/game.js row from the first-round finders was refuted. The hot JS paths (physics, collide, net, per-car
  draw) were read in full and are already pooled.
- The hunt-6 open list (pickFor, noiseBurst, interp/inbox copies, fitHud, hud-tyres, windFromSpeed) went to three
  worker subagents in worktrees, on disjoint paths, and was merged here.
- A read-only trace of the iPhone GLX and TLX-WebGL2 reports.
- Every count below comes from a behavioural test that runs the shipped code and is red on the old code.
