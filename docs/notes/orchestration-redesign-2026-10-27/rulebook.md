## 4. The de-facto model

Bryce opens one tiny "seed" session per fleet (A = his first Claude account, B = his second, C = Codex, D = Cursor/Grok "DAD") and types the fleet's grants into it or into the first orchestrator. The orchestrator (Opus, auto mode, booted from the deploy tip) reads ~13k tokens of rulebook on top of a ~100k base, posts `ORCH <L> LIVE` on a git-branch bus, arms a bus Monitor plus a 50–55 min timer plus one hourly cron, picks `ready` issues, and launches sibling cloud sessions by role — workers (one task, OWNED globs), hunters (find-and-fix rounds), owners (one PR to merged), sync lanes, reviewers, a fleet watcher — each with a ~40-line brief that quotes Bryce's words with timestamps and ends `STOP AT: 150k`. Every lane pays the same ~100k boot. A worker claims paths on a second git branch, pushes `cursor/<topic>-<hash>` at its first commit, opens a DRAFT PR whose body is the log (Plan checklist, fleet block, `fleet-status:` line mirrored as a comment), subscribes to the PR and arms a timer, iterates to green, runs `ready-gate.mjs`, flips the draft, and sends `READY <PR> <sha>` to its fleet letter via `agent-msg.mjs` (which mirrors to the bus and, cross-account, fires a routine that spawns a fresh session to repost the line). The orchestrator wakes, runs `merge-gate.mjs` (open, no `MERGE-HOLD:`, ready receipt, CI check, gen:check on a throw-away merge, and for game PRs a bus `GAME-SLOT` plus a green Pages run since the last game `DONE`), merges with a hook-pinned receipt, posts `DONE` with a `Merged-by:` trailer, dispatches Pages if none is queued, and archives the finished session by `fleet-archive.mjs`. Non-Claude hosts see only the bus, the PR body and PR comments (a workflow mirrors `to:#<PR>` bus lines into comments).

A session dies three ways: it reaches its prose cap (150k; orchestrator 300k) and must hand off; it hits the account quota and parks with one `send_later` at the reset; or it is archived after its PR merges. An orchestrator at its cap deletes its own timers, writes a ≤40-line brief to a pushed `cursor/` branch, sends `SPAWN ORCH <L> <branch>:<path>` to the seed, which checks quota and `create_session`s a successor at depth 1; the successor posts `ORCH <L> LIVE`, re-arms the three wake sources, and archives its predecessor; workers find the new orchestrator by `fleet-bus.mjs who <L>` at send time. Escalations go up as `BLOCKED` (bus + `@orchestrator` comment + `send_message`); anything "the user's call" is relayed to Bryce in the orchestrator's chat and comes back as a relaunch, never as a message, because a message carries no authority. Measured outcome (FLEET-B-TRANSCRIPT-AUDIT:286; SYSTEM-AUDIT-4:130-142): 7 orchestrators in 4.5 h at 40 % of fleet spend, ~$18 per merged PR, check-ins firing every 15–17 min with 0 of 22 acting, and READY→merge of 203 min for game PRs.

## 6. The eight biggest design problems

1. **Rotation is the engine of the rulebook.** Sessions are designed to die at a prose cap and be reborn through a seed, a bus line, a brief file and a title rename; that one choice generates rules 2-4, 8-9 of §3, the `who <L>` indirection, the successor-archive rule, and 7 orchestrators in 4.5 h at 40 % of spend (FLEET-B:286). Platform: per-wake cache-read cost is real (FLEET-COST:111-117) — but the plan's own principle "never rotates because of chat length" (fleet-redesign.md:43) was dropped; HARNESS-SETTINGS:55-57 and fleet-redesign.md §4A (Projects) both remove rotation. Design.
2. **Authority is modelled as Bryce's typed words.** Every scope change is a relaunch with a UTC-stamped quote (hard lines 7, 8, 13, 15), grants cannot travel by message, and ~18 touchpoints need him. The platform fact is narrow — the classifier strips tool results, so only `.claude/**` writes and bypass flags were ever refused (fleet-redesign.md:10-19) — and the design generalised it to all authority. Design, on a small platform base.
3. **Communication has no schema.** ≈ 30 channels, ≈ 50 typed tokens, five meanings of HANDOFF, a `to:*` bus everyone must grep-filter, two overlapping maps (comms.md / communication.md), and a tool that now refuses lines two role cards prescribe (fleet-bus.mjs:149-153 vs role-seed.md:146). Platform: `send_message` is same-account and Cursor/Codex cannot message out (MULTI-AGENT:394-397; communication.md:267); the git-log-as-bus with free-text bodies is design.
4. **Wake policy is prose, layered, and contradicted by practice.** 12 cadence prescriptions, "four signals, nothing else" beside a bus Monitor and an hourly cron, a 60 s watch beside "no tick"; transcripts show 15–17 min check-ins, 3-min ticks re-armed, and a dead routine burning $15.80 (SYSTEM-AUDIT-4:139-142). Platform: subscriptions drop events (MULTI-AGENT:364-367) and the 1 h cache (HARNESS-SETTINGS:147); the layering is design.
5. **The rulebook is too big to follow.** 27k words in scope, a 12–16.5k-word orchestrator boot set on a ~100k base, 186 "never"s, 16 hard lines + 6 first acts + 3 hand-off steps in one file — while RULEBOOK-REWRITE:192-195 itself cites IFScale ("68 % at 500 instructions") and "under 200 lines". Design.
6. **Roles multiply sessions and loops.** Nine-plus session roles where the plan wanted four (fleet-redesign.md:41 vs launch-policy.md:369); owner/worker/hunter/sync are variants of one job with four written loops (worker.md, agents/worker.md, role-pr-owner §steward loop, steward); each extra sibling pays ~98k boot (launch-policy.md:381-382; FLEET-B row 17). Platform: the boot cost is platform; multiplying it is design.
7. **Merge pacing is a free-text state machine with blind spots.** GAME-SLOT TTLs, INCIDENT freezes, Pages-train state, Bryce's UI merges (invisible until merge-attribution), lent PRs with no merge authority (FLEET-B row 5), ABSENT-fleet adoption rules in a dated note that contradict AGENTS.md (SYSTEM-AUDIT-3:118-121); result READY→merge 203 min for game PRs (SYSTEM-AUDIT-3:82). Platform: one GitHub identity and the Pages concurrency group (multi-host.md:401; FLEET-ADAPTIVE:107-110); N peer mergers sharing one slot is design.
8. **Enforcement and prose have drifted apart.** Hook-gated actions (merge/ready/launch receipts, context-cap) coexist with prose caps that overshoot by 2× (FLEET-B row 12), retired mechanisms still taught or coded (lease `@doc`, Merge Desk in fleet-archive.mjs:34, the stale routine), ghosts (`ReadNotifications`, `INCIDENT-<date>.md`, `pages-red-triage`), and a guard test that pins sentence copies rather than behaviour (rulebook-guards.test.mjs:6). Design: rules should live in tools and hooks with one pointer sentence, not in N pointer paragraphs.
