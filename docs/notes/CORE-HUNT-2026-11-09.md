# Core hunt — 2026-11-09
Kind: measurement · Re-measure-by: 2027-02-09

Hunt for the bugs a player hits in a normal race, on deploy tip `e808230`.

Method: `prior-art-scout` first, then the real `js/game.js` in the Node VM (`tools/lib/game-vm.cjs`):
full AI races with per-tick invariant checks, a player-driven race, a restart state diff, and wet-start

No browser was used, so nothing here is evidence about rendering, the HUD or touch input.

## Confirmed and fixed in this PR

### 1. Rain with TYRE WEAR off: the player is stranded on garage slicks (race-breaking)
- **Repro (VM):** `createGame({seat:"shipped", storage:{tyreWear:"off"}})`, `race("silverstone","day","rain")`.
  Before: player `tyre S, tread 0, gripMult 0.72`; AI `tread null, gripMult 0.97`; `pits.info().enabled false`.
  `wet` weather: 0.82 against 0.94. Same with the pinned McLaren seat.
- **Mechanism:** `gridUp` fits `tyres.startRecord(c, tread)` (`js/game.js:1788`). For a car with a
  garage tyre (`tyreOpt`: the player, a MY TEAM mate) `startRecord` returned that tyre whatever the
  weather (`js/physics/tyre-model.js:724`). The AI field carries the `tread: null` "right tyre" sentinel.
  `PitLane.enabled()` needs wear on (`js/race/pit-lane.js:83`), so with wear off there is no stop.
- **Fix:** the session's `startRecord` fits the weather's tread class when wear is off and the car's
  own set is a slick. A treaded set of the player's own is kept. Wear on is unchanged.
- **Test:** `tests/unit/tyre-model.test.mjs` "wear OFF: a car on its own garage slick…" (fails on the old source).
- **After:** player `tread 2, 0.97` in rain and `tread 1, 0.94` in the wet, both seats.
- **Not fixed (#5253, open):** a dry start that turns to rain mid-race with wear off strands the player
  the same way. A first fix was reverted; see "Follow-up, 2026-11-10" below.

### 2. Tyre "laps left" read up to a lap short; the AI field boxed wet-for-wet on it
- **Repro (VM):** a full race in the VM, monaco, rain, seed 7, 5 laps, wear `real`, player handed
  to the AI: all 21 AI stopped once, wet to wet, at wear 0.38-0.57 with `pitWhy "plan"` on a plan of
  0 stops. Singapore rain seed 5: the same. A set wearing 0.19 a lap would have finished at about 0.95.
- **Mechanism:** `lapsLeft` computed `life = lapsOn / wear` with whole-lap `lapsOn` (`physicalLaps` floors)
  and continuous wear, so late in a lap the rate read up to `(n+1)/n` high
  (`js/physics/tyre-model.js:995`). `wetLapsToStop` then compared it with `lapsToFlag`, which counts the
  current lap whole (`js/race/pit-lane.js:1519`). The HUD's laps-left reads the same function.
- **Fix:** `lapsLeft` counts the part-lap driven; `wetLapsToStop` takes the driven part of the lap off `left`.
- **Tests:** `tyre-model.test.mjs` "lapsLeft measures the wear rate…", `pit-lane.test.mjs`
  "wetLapsToStop counts what is LEFT of the lap…" (the second fails on the old source; the existing
  pinned-numbers test now states its car is at the line).
- **After:** monaco rain seed 7: 0 AI stops.
- **Re-run by the adopting session (2026-11-10, base worktree against this branch, same script):**
  monaco rain seed 7, 5 laps: 21 stops → 0 (sets end at wear 0.85-0.93). 14 laps: 21 → 0.
  singapore rain seed 5, 5 laps: 21 → **8**. The 13 that stay out finish at wear 0.92-0.95, so
  the 8 are cars whose set reads marginally short of the flag. The fix is partial there; the
  residual is filed as its own issue, not claimed fixed.

### 3. A stale ceremony arm made SKIP TO RESULTS play the ceremony (low)
- **Mechanism:** `update()` arms `RaceCeremony.want(true)` at every natural flag (`js/game.js:4703`);
  `endRace` returns before `afterClassify` in qualifying and time trial (`js/game.js:2926-2942`), and
  nothing else cleared `_want`. The next race's `skipToResults()` → `endRace()` then started the ceremony.
- **Fix:** `RaceCeremony.resetLive()` disarms (`startRaceBody` and `quitToMenu` already call it).
- **Test:** `tests/unit/race-ceremony.test.mjs` "resetLive disarms a stale want" (fails on the old source).
- Code-traced and unit-tested; not reproduced in a browser.

## Follow-up, 2026-11-10 (the adopting session)

### 4. CHANGEABLE weather with TYRE WEAR off strands the player when the rain arrives (race-breaking, #5253)
- **Repro (VM):** `createGame({seat:"shipped", storage:{tyreWear:"off"}})`, `race("silverstone","day","dry",{laps:5})`,
  `__apex.weatherArc("dry","rain",20)`, step 60 s. Before: player `S, tread 0, gripMult 0.72`; AI `tread null, 0.97`;
  `pits.info().enabled false`, for the rest of the race.
- **Mechanism:** the AI's `tread: null` sentinel reads the weather's tread every step (`gripMult`); the player's
  explicit tread only changes at a stop, and wear off has no pit lane.
- **Attempted and reverted (PR #5272, `f3c63b3`, reverted by the next session):** `followWeather` in the tyre
  session's `update` re-fitted a wear-off car on its own garage slick to the weather's class record every tick.
  Two unit tests that pass without it failed with it, both from the same design (rewriting `c.tread` and `c.mods`
  in flight): `mechanics-integration-vm` "weather grip is continuous…" (`G.records.current()` changes mid-race,
  because `js/race/session-records.js` `config()` keys the record on `c.tread` and `c.mods`), and
  `player-dynamics-vm` "wheel state ON: … the stop gets longer" (the test pins `P.tread = 0` in a storm; the next
  tick re-fitted wets, so the slick-in-a-storm stop the test asks for never happened). With only the
  `tyre-model.js` hunk reverted both files pass, 39/39.
- **What a redesign must hold:** the record key stays fixed through the race (no `c.tread`/`c.mods` writes),
  and an explicit tread a caller sets is honoured. Reading the weather's tread inside `gripMult` /
  `aquaInto` for a wear-off car on its garage slick meets the first but not the second unless the
  player-dynamics fixture states its intent (wear on, or a flag); that is a design call for #5253.

### Pace on a fitted wet set (measured, 2026-11-10)
- **Question:** a WET class record carries stat mods on the player (`speed 0.88`, `accel 0.90`,
  `cornering 0.90`, `braking 0.94` on top of the car's own) while an AI pays its wet cost only through the
  record's `off` (`-0.020` on vmax, wear on) or nothing at all (wear off: `AiDrive.tyrePace` on its dry class).
- **Measured (VM, monza, rain, 10 s full throttle from a standing start, other cars parked):** the player as
  fitted at the lights (`W`, `mods.speed 0.871`) against the same car with the AI's treatment (its garage
  record, `tread: null`, the same 0.97 grip column): wear off 193.5 m / 119.6 km/h against 216.3 m / 134.1;
  wear on 178.7 m / 110.6 against 202.1 m / 125.5. About 11 % less ground for the player on the same grip.
- **So:** fixing the tyre at the lights gives grip parity (0.97 both) and not pace parity. The gap predates
  this note (any player who stops for wets carries it). Filed as a follow-up; not changed here.

### Decisions and drops
- **Wear ON rain start** (player on the garage slick, 21 AI on wets): re-measured, 0.72 against 0.97 on both
  seats. A design call, #5251. **Decided by Bryce 2026-11-10 (option A):** the lights fit the weather's tread
  when the road is wet and the garage set is a slick, wear on or off; a treaded garage set is kept. After:
  player `W, tread 2, 0.97`, the same as the AI. The test that pinned the old behaviour as intended
  ("wear on: the player's own set, whatever the sky") was replaced, not relaxed.
- **Fix 2 is partial at Singapore** (8 of 21 still box wet-for-wet): #5252.
- **`frozen` after RESTART then quit: dropped.** Every reader of `frozen` is gated on `state` race/count or on
  `skyViewOverride` (`js/game.js` tickBody and render, `js/ui/hud.js:1814`, `js/render/shared/mirror-pass.js`),
  and `startRaceBody` clears it at the next committed start. No effect a player can see was found.
- **Caution re-raise cycle: dropped as a bug.** The re-raise after the 45 s hold is pinned as intended
  (`tests/unit/race-control.test.mjs` "after the hold, a persistent hazard re-raises"). Shards and broken panels
  despawn after 4 s and 6 s asleep; only displaced cones persist, and a local yellow needs three of them asleep on
  the racing surface in one sector. That needs Rapier, which the VM does not run. Not reproduced.
- **Rescue into a stopped car: dropped.** The precondition (a stationary, non-retired car on the centreline)
  arises only inside a Rapier incident. Not runnable headless, not reproduced.

## Checked, no defect found

- 9 full 5-lap AI races (monza ×5, monaco rain, spa storm, baku, singapore rain, suzuka): no
  non-finite car state, `energy` and `tyreWear` in range, no car stuck over 3 s, no backward `prog` jump.
- Determinism: monza seed 42 run twice gave identical classifications and times.
- Frame rate: physics is a fixed 1/60 step with a 5-step cap (`js/game.js:8610-8631`), so play is
  frame-rate independent down to 12 fps. A VM run at `step(1/30)` or `step(1/120)` is a different
  simulation and not something a player can reach.
- Restart: a race restarted after 150 s of penalties, damage, a retirement and a pit arm matched a
  fresh start in every per-car and session field except the per-race seed round.
- Player-driven 3-lap monza: lapped finish flagged at the next crossing, +5 s ladder applied, P22.
- Curvature channels: 15 read sites classified, no leak (`curvature-channels` and `vstd-invariant` pass).
- Refuted: "a retiree is credited a lap in `RaceControl.classify`": a retiree that completed the same
  laps crossed the line before the lapped finisher took the flag, so it ranks ahead by FIA order.
- Stale ledger row: "race-mode `c.best` counts a cut lap" is no longer true (`js/game.js:5643`).
