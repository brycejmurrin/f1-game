## Verdict: the slice is lean
Nothing here costs a frame during a race on the iPhone with default settings:
- **Net** (`snapshot.js`, `netplay.js`, `interp.js`). Encoding counts first, then writes one exact-size buffer at
  13 B per car with 16-bit quantisation. Publishing is a fixed 20 Hz, one encode shared by every guest, with
  scratch arrays and pooled relay entries. Decoding happens once and the decoded entry is adopted without a copy. The
  interp ring is bounded at 32. Sampling in `tick()` writes into per-remote scratch objects.
- **Perf instrumentation**: `PerfSpans` is off by default; when on, it uses a preallocated Float64Array. `LoopHealth`
  has no allocation. `RenderRate` is scalar. The `PerfGov.tick` ring needs no sort. `ThermalDrift`,
  `MetricsOverlay` and `PhoneRecorder` are opt-in, and their loops exit when off.
- **Overlay and share**: the publisher runs at 250 ms on a timer, only while it has peers. The feed diffs fields
  without `JSON.stringify`. Rendering is transform-only. The share card caches its font and QR load, uses an
  OffscreenCanvas, and builds nothing until a chip is tapped.
- **Workers**: the strip decode transfers `pix.buffer` and reuses one worker. Every pending entry settles.
- **Data Hub**: requests to the same URL share one in-flight promise. OpenF1 has a 28/min sliding window, a 400 ms
  gap and 2 bodies at once, and Jolpica runs on its own lane. Standings fetch in parallel. Tabs reuse their nodes.
  Telemetry paints blit cached bases, and its readout writes skip text that has not changed.
