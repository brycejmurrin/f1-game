# Scandinavian Raceway — Visual Design Brief

**Setting:** DAY, green theme (Småland pine and an airfield). ~4.02 km, 8 turns, clockwise.

## 1. Setting

Built around a working airfield in the Småland pine forest. The long back straight IS the runway — wide, flat, and conspicuously wider than the rest of the lap. Six Swedish Grands Prix in the 1970s; nothing since. Flat, fast and strange, with dense pine right up to the edge everywhere except the runway.

## 2. Atmosphere & palette

Northern light: cool, blue-shifted, long shadows even at midday. Pine is dark and desaturated. Sand run-off is pale grey, not golden.

## 3. Elevation

Flat. This is the flattest circuit in the roster — do not add relief.

## 5. Track features

- The runway back straight, noticeably wider than the rest of the lap.
- A long, long final corner onto the pit straight.
- Dense pine everywhere else.

## 6. Modelling notes

- The width change at the runway is the identifying feature. Make it visible.
- Scots pine in dense ranks — tall bare trunks, canopy only at the top.
- Low wooden and corrugated buildings, nothing grand.
