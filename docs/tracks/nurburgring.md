# Nürburgring GP-Strecke — Visual Design Brief

**Setting:** DAY, green theme (Eifel upland forest). ~5.15 km, 15 turns, clockwise.

> This is the modern **Grand-Prix-Strecke** only — the 5.1 km circuit built in 1984 beside the old start/finish. It is NOT the Nordschleife. Nothing in this brief should imply a 20 km lap through the mountains.

## 1. Setting
600 m up in the Eifel volcanic uplands, west of Koblenz. Cold, damp, permanently overcast-leaning light over dark spruce plantation and open moorland grass. The circuit is a compact modern autodrome dropped into a landscape that is anything but modern: heavy grey masonry, steep slate roofs, and the ruined 12th-century **Burg Nürburg** on its volcanic cone, visible from most of the lap and the thing the whole place is named after. Bolted onto the back of the paddock is the 2009 **ring°boulevard** — glass, steel and barrel vaults, the one genuinely new object here.

## 2. Atmosphere & palette
Muted, washed, high-haze. Saturated colour is wrong for this circuit; even the sponsor boards are dulled.
- Sky: zenith `[0.38, 0.48, 0.60]`, horizon `[0.66, 0.70, 0.72]`; sun a colourless `[0.92, 0.93, 0.92]`
- Fog `[0.62, 0.66, 0.68]` at density ≈0.0030 — heavier than most tracks, let distance fade early
- Spruce: `[0.07, 0.22, 0.12]` deep, `[0.09, 0.27, 0.14]` mid; broadleaf `[0.17, 0.40, 0.19]`
- Grass `[0.16, 0.36, 0.17]`; gravel `[0.62, 0.58, 0.48]`
- Eifel masonry: stone `[0.60, 0.60, 0.59]`, shadowed `[0.47, 0.47, 0.47]`, slate `[0.30, 0.31, 0.35]`
- Muted hoarding primaries: `[0.72, 0.20, 0.18]`, `[0.16, 0.30, 0.54]`, `[0.80, 0.70, 0.16]`

## 3. Elevation
Real upland relief — the lap falls away from the Arena and climbs hard back to the pits.
- s≈0.00–0.10: level pit straight into the Castrol-S and the Mercedes-Arena.
- s≈0.18: **8 m drop** out of the Arena through the Ford Kurve.
- s≈0.42: low ground through the back loop, ~12 m below the line.
- s≈0.66: **Dunlop-Kehre** rise (~6 m).
- s≈0.86: long ~11 m **climb** back to the Veedol chicane and the pit straight.

## 5. Track features
- The Mercedes-Arena: three slow corners inside a grandstand bowl, half-width pinched to 6.2 m.
- Banked corners are gentle: Castrol-S 3.5°, Ford Kurve 4.5°, Bit-Kurve 3°, Schumacher-S 4°.
- Dunlop-Kehre and the Veedol chicane both narrow to ~6.3–6.4 m.
- Proper gravel traps at Dunlop, Schumacher-S and Veedol; armco everywhere through the forest.

## 6. Modelling notes
- The back-section stands must be OPEN scaffold — tube legs, plank decks, painted bench rows, no roof and no back wall. Seeing spruce through a grandstand is the Eifel look; a clad shell is not.
- Give the pit block masonry cues a modern autodrome never has: the pier arcade, the eaves shadow line, twin steep slate pitches.
- Keep everything desaturated and let fog eat distance early; a bright Nürburgring is a wrong Nürburgring.
- Burg Nürburg goes on its own hill in the backdrop ring so it sits ABOVE the treeline, not in it — it is the one landmark no other circuit can borrow.
- The Dunlop bridge is recognised by its arch ribs and the yellow board hung off them, not by the deck: model those, and put the board above the clearance envelope.
- Close the Arena with continuous seat walls; gaps between stand boxes turn the bowl back into an open field.
