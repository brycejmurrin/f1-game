# Fuji Speedway — Visual Design Brief

**Setting:** DAY, green theme (conifer plateau under a volcano). ~4.55 km, 16 turns, clockwise.

## 1. Setting

A conifer plateau on the southern flank of Mount Fuji, 580 m up. Two things identify it and nothing else needs to: the 1.475 km pit straight, the longest in this roster, and the mountain itself — a snow-capped cone due north that dominates every frame looking back down the straight. The paddock is low, dark and modern; the outfield is dense Japanese cedar.

## 2. Atmosphere & palette

Cool, high-altitude light. Cedar foliage is dark and blue-shifted, not the warm green of a European park. Keep the horizon pale and slightly hazy so the mountain reads as distant.

## 3. Elevation

Rises through the esses on the back half and falls all the way down the pit straight — roughly 40 m of total change, most of it gradual.

## 5. Track features

- The pit straight is the feature. Give it length and nothing to break the eye.
- Turn 1 is a long, slow right at the end of it.
- The sector-2 esses are tight and tree-lined.
- 100R and the Netz corner close the lap with a hairpin.

## 6. Modelling notes

- Mount Fuji is the single most identifying object. It belongs on the northern skyline as a large, low-frequency cone, snow-capped, never a generic ridge.
- Cedar (sugi) is the tree: tall, narrow, dark. Plant in dense ranks, not scattered.
- The grandstands along the pit straight are modern, dark-clad and continuous.
