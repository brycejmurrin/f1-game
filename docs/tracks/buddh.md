# Buddh International Circuit — Visual Design Brief

**Setting:** DAY, modern theme (Greater Noida plain). ~5.14 km, 16 turns, clockwise.

## 1. Setting

Tilke-designed on flat farmland outside Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. Three Indian Grands Prix, 2011 to 2013, then lost to a tax dispute. Wide, modern, and built with deliberate artificial elevation on an otherwise flat plain — the Turn 10-11 double-apex sits at the bottom of a constructed drop.

## 2. Atmosphere & palette

Hot, hazy, dust-shifted. The light is diffused by haze rather than clean. Grass is dry and patchy; earth is reddish.

## 3. Elevation

Engineered rather than natural: a climb into Turn 3 and a long drop to the Turn 10-11 complex. Read as banked earthworks, not hills.

## 4. Landmarks & surroundings by lap position

Clockwise, so **+1 (right of travel) is the infield** — pits, paddock, broadcast.
The outfield (-1) is open farmland almost the whole way round.

## 5. Track features

- A long back straight into a heavy braking zone.
- The Turn 10-11 double-apex right, taken at increasing radius.
- Very wide tarmac run-off throughout.

## 6. Modelling notes

- The main grandstand is large, modern and the only substantial structure.
- The surroundings are flat farmland and scrub — keep the outfield low and open.
- Haze on the horizon is a defining quality of every photograph of this place.
