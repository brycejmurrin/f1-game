### Refuted (do not re-report)

- "HUD (`hud.js`, `hud-layout.js`) is race-only, so it can be lazy": `GameHud.create(G)` runs at top level in `game.js:3495`, and five `js/ui` modules reference it.
- "`collide.js`, `tyre-model.js` and `ai-drive.js` are race-step only": `Collide.create` runs at top level (`game.js:4164`). `TyreModel` is read by lobby settings, race settings, forecast, weather arc and pit lane.

### Low / no action

- `js/core/script-loader.js:164-169`: a lazy `<script>` that loads successfully stays in `<head>`. Non-strict callers re-fetch after a registry clear (`:111-113`), but no caller in `js/track` or `js/core` evicts a circuit, so elements do not accumulate per race. A retained element costs bytes; the engine keeps the source text while the script's functions live, whether or not the element is removed.
- `js/perf/metrics-overlay.js:1142-1146` builds its submenu at 0, 250 and 1000 ms, but the guard at `:1092` makes each retry about 6 `getElementById` calls.
- `js/car/ghost.js` parses its store (up to 512 KB) once on the first `setTrack` and memoises it (`loadStore`). This is already fixed.

### Clean (checked, nothing to do)

- Pack rungs: a phone takes the 128 px strips (144 KB albedo + 425 KB normal), not 256 px (`js/render/shared/assets.js:640-642`). The decode is a streaming PNG reader with one task per layer. TLX nulls the CPU copy after upload (`js/render/three/tlx.js:5361-5364`). Mipmaps are generated on the GPU.
- Re-encoding the strips: the shipped bake is already adaptive-filter zlib 9. Forced filters came out larger (normal-128: 469 KB best against 425 KB shipped). The palette output was 100 KB, but it is lossy (164,281 distinct colours) and fails the loader's RGBA check. Rejected.
- Models: fetched per circuit (`loadModelsFor`), 0.2 MB in total. Stills: on demand. Fonts: 3 × ~12 KB preloaded, correctly. `assets/atlases` (15 MB) and `assets/pack/webbake.js` are author-time files, never fetched.
- Memory lifecycle: `quitToMenu` / `releaseFieldScratch`, the garage LRU slots, the pit-crew rig cap, the `race-facts`, coach and radio log caps, and the livery swatch caps are all bounded. No per-cycle growth source was found in `js/race`, `js/garage`, `js/core` or `game.js`. This agrees with the hunt 7 mem-census (+0.8 to +1.3 MB on the last cycle).
