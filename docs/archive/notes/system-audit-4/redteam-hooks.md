## Clean (checked, holds)
- The deploy-push guard catches literal refspecs, `refs/heads/`, `eval`, `bash -c`, `-c alias.x='!…'` and the `--force` abbreviations.
- `.githooks/pre-push` is a real second layer. In the sandbox it caught every push form that bash-guard missed, unless the hook itself is skipped (R1).
- protect-files catches generated-file writes through redirects, sed/perl/awk -i, tee, cp, mv, dd, truncate, rsync, install, ln, git checkout/restore from a ref, `sh -c`, heredocs, `..` and absolute paths, and @gen-shell blocks.
- The ratchet ban catches `./`, `cd`, `$U` and `=` forms of `--update`. CI runs `ratchets.mjs --base` (ci.yml:495, max raise 40), which caps any hand raise.
- The subagent browser ban covers cli.js, `.bin/playwright`, `npm run-script`/`exec`, `npx -y`, `pnpm exec`, `nohup &` and tools/shot.
- github-mcp-guard denies squash, a missing receipt, and `draft:"False"`.
- With python3 missing, protect-files, github-mcp-guard, mcp-browser-guard, context-cap and subagent-contract exit 127, and `onFailure:"block"` turns that into a block (hooks doc above). Cursor's shell-guard denies on any odd exit code.
