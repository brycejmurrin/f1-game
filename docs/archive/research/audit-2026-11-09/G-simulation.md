## 1. Summary

- The simulation is an arcade "Frenet-lane" model, not a rigid-body sim: every car lives in track coordinates (s along the centreline, x lateral). AI cars integrate s/x directly on speed caps; only the human car runs a per-axle bicycle model in world space and is projected back to (s, x) each step (`trackFrom`). Collisions, walls and laps are all resolved in (s, x).
- Timestep is a fixed 1/60 s with an accumulator, wall dt clamped to 0.25 s, at most 5 steps a frame with the backlog dropped. Physics is therefore identical at 30/60/120 Hz displays; timers all run on sim time. No spiral of death. This part is sound.
- It is deliberately not F1-scale. Measured at the shipped default (OVERALL SPEED notch 11, PACE 0.84): top speed 216 km/h, 0–100 km/h 6.4 s, braking 2.2 g, cornering 2.2 g peak; AI best laps are 1.29–1.54× the real pole lap, and the ratio is not constant across circuits.
- Health of the core: good. 37 headless races on 16 circuits (3 to 57 laps, incl. one full Grand Prix distance): zero NaN, zero cars stuck >10 s, zero lap-counter regressions, bit-identical results for the same seed, a car at rest stays at rest (flat and on a 2.6 % slope), reverse-and-recross of the line does not inflate laps, bad dt / NaN steer do not poison state.
- Real defects found are edge-of-envelope, not core: a race can start with no player car after a team switch via share code; grip drops 8.7 % in one tick when a weather transition ends; `trackFrom` settles on a wrong arc position beyond the inside edge of tight corners; the AI line at Spa earns the AI its own track-limit penalties; a few latch/guard bugs (BOOST on empty battery, sector split after reversing, qualifying NO TIME after a lap-0 rescue).
- The stated physics contract (no curvature/racing line reaches the unassisted player) HOLDS in the code and in a behavioural probe on 3 circuits. The test that guards it does not: it is a count of `.curvature(` call sites per file and still passes 3/3 with three deliberate violations injected.

- Career arithmetic checked out under a 12-season scripted run (points sum 2424 = 24×101 every season, money finite and non-negative); findings there are validation gaps, not arithmetic errors.
