## 1. Summary

- **What this part of the project actually is.** A static, no-build game whose three renderers (three.js r187
  WebGPU/WebGL2 — r187 confirmed from the bundle and hash-matched to npm; a hand-written WebGL2; a hand-written,
  opt-in WebGPU) and platform layers (Web Audio, touch/tilt/gamepad, WebRTC over public Nostr relays, a service
  worker with hashed precache) are, on the whole, carefully feature-tested. On the owner's path (iPhone, Safari,
  three WebGPU) no P0 was found: audio unlock, touch release nets, wake lock re-acquire, storage guards, CSP hashes,
  precache integrity and feed error handling all checked out against the specs.
- **Platform health: good core, wrong edges.** The real defects sit where the code leans on library internals or
  deploy tooling: the race-entry warm's "1x1 scissor" is inert on r187 (full-resolution warm on the iPhone path; the
  unit test asserts a flag three ignores); the deploy stamp omits two lazy bundles so the garage exterior and debug
  overlay can never load on the live site; the opt-in WGX path has a pipeline/bind-group mismatch with upscaling on;
  desktop three-WebGPU can hit a caught TypeError after chunk arrays are nulled; a guest can answer a stale replayed
  offer on a re-used room code; an abandoned ICE restart is never retried.
- **External feeds: shapes match today.** Every Jolpica/OpenF1 field the code reads exists (confirmed by live GETs);
  boot, menus, Season and Career never touch the feeds. Edge defects: a mid-season mover is shown under the team he
  left (`Constructors[0]`), no last-season fallback for the schedule after 1 January, four hub tabs gate on a
  latest-session call that OpenF1 locks during live sessions.
- **Real-world data: the roster is right, the circuits are not.** All 11 teams, 22 drivers, numbers, PU suppliers,
  race/sprint points and the opt-in `REAL_2026` calendar verified correct. But the DEFAULT season is not the 2026
  calendar (24 rounds incl. Sakhir/Jeddah/Istanbul/Portimão; the real season ran 23), and circuit data has a systemic
  problem: `def.turns` is curvature-peak-picked yet shown to players as FIA turn numbers; start lines are on the
  wrong straight at Donington, Brands Hatch, Jerez (and probably Zolder); straight-mode zones are wrong in count or
  place at Monza, Singapore, Spa, Shanghai, Zandvoort; pits are on the wrong side at Spa, Lusail, Zandvoort, Suzuka,
  Hungaroring, Interlagos; Madrid runs 56 laps (real 57) and has its banking on the wrong corner. Built lap lengths
  are sound (all but three within 2 %). There is NO lap-record, lat/long, time-zone or per-circuit pit-limit table in
  the game, so those could not be wrong; nothing there is invented.
- **Tests and ledger are not ground truth here.** At least eight tests pin a wrong external fact or a mechanism the
  platform ignores (shortened-race points P4, Monza 2 zones, Baku night, Spotify `product`, the scissor flag,
  `adapter.isFallbackAdapter`, Qatar 1 zone, the AudioContext rebuild). DEFECT-LEDGER lists the Madrid lap count
  under a FIXED entry; it is not fixed. docs/BUGS.md is 12 lines and covered none of this.
_(several lines withheld: security detail, kept in the owner's private page)_
  patches; licence files shipped). No secrets in the tree.
- **Totals:** 163 findings after verification — 0 P0, 21 P1, 62 P2, 80 P3; 157 VERIFIED, 6 UNVERIFIED; 3 claims
  refuted and dropped (CIRC1-08 Albert Park zones; sub-claims AUDIO-04b, FEEDS-11b). Almost all NEW.
