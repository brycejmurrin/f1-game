## Verdict

The tree came through the campaign structurally sound: with the previous 197
findings landed, a fresh 19-lens sweep still finds only 16 high-severity
issues, and just 12 of those are behavior rather than prose — the guard
suites, the engine/data split and the G-façade discipline are all holding.
What the sweep did surface is that the well is not dry, it has changed
composition: the remaining mess is no longer scattered comment drift but two
**genera repeated across files**, and both are geometry. Eight sites hand
centre-anchored offsets to base-anchored primitives (`addCyl`/`addPrism`/
`addCone`), which is why Zandvoort's watertoren floats 10 m, Nürburg's slate
cap is buried mid-shaft, and every camera tower's legs hang clear of the
ground; two more sites omit `def._sceneryShift`, putting Suzuka's bridge
pillars two-thirds of a lap from the deck they support — the same class of bug
as the `bankZones` remap this session fixed in `ed5a310f`, found
independently. By directory the concentration is `js/track/` 19 (9 bugs),
`tools/` 17 across 15 files, `docs/` 16 (all prose, mostly stale counts),
`js/circuits/` 14 and `js/game/` 13 — i.e. the scenery emitters and the tool
index are where a reader is most likely to be misled today. The `tools/`
pocket has its own signature worth naming: three tools describe or measure
something other than what they do (`ui-mcp-survey` drives Playwright not MCP,
`wait-polling-lint` is blind to the entire `tools/` tree it claims to scan,
`pick-tests` silently drops the first path you hand it), which makes them
worse than absent because their output reads as authoritative.
