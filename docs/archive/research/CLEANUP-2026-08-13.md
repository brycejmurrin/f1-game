## Discovery headlines

### bankZones were left on the wrong corners by the start-line rotation

The session's biggest defect, and it was found by attributing a *foundation
spec* red rather than by reading code. An earlier commit, `7a173519`, rotated
the start-line frame and compensated every fraction-keyed table with
`def._sceneryShift` — except one. `bankingProfile()` (`js/track/mesh.js`) reads
`z.frac` **raw**, so **91 of 141 authored bank zones sat on the wrong corners
across 25 of the 27 rotated circuits**. Zandvoort's 19° Luyendyk bowl — a 4.82 m
lift — was applied to a dead straight; Indianapolis's 9° oval Turn 1 sat off its
turn. This is not cosmetic: grip (`bankMu`) and body roll both read the profile,
so the driving model had been banking the wrong metres of road.

Fixed by applying the same shift the rest of the engine already applies
(`ed5a310f`), with reversed-frame circuits (monaco, paul_ricard, singapore)
spot-checked explicitly. Consequences worked through in the same wave:

- The **physics characterization golden** had to be regenerated (`4691c765`) —
  the prior baseline was generated at a descendant of the rotation and had
  embedded Monza's misplaced Lesmo-window lift (~8% mu in two scenario paths).
  The regeneration was **not** accepted as a blanket re-bake: straight-line
  accel and off-track recovery are byte-identical, and only steady corner load
  and trail-brake-into-rotation moved.
- **Qatar's probe tolerance was calibrated against the misplaced zone** and was
  passing by 0.007 m. It was re-measured after the fix and retightened to the
  shared contract (`fb3c2c3f`); the merge order was revised so the bankZones
  fix landed *before* the foundation re-pins that depend on it.
- **Collateral, recorded not fixed:** `76da73db`'s qatar tolerance and the
  Montreal footbridge / Monza Lesmo tunings were all calibrated bank-blind, and
  `js/track/surface.js`'s `pyMin` comment describes the defective placement.

### The anchor genus, and the Suzuka shift

W4 found the same shape twice more, independently of the bankZones work.
**Genus:** `addCyl`/`addPrism`/`addCone`/`addFrustum` build **upward from `c`**,
and eight authoring sites were written as if the primitive centred its anchor —
which is why Zandvoort's watertoren floats 10 m with its cap buried in the drum,
Nürburg's slate cap sits mid-shaft, and every camera tower's legs hang clear of
the ground. Fixed in one measured pass (`7d63c24c`), before/after positionally
aligned primitive dumps, zero misaligned lines, no primitive gained or lost.
**Suzuka:** two more sites omit `def._sceneryShift` outright, putting the
bridge pillars two-thirds of a lap from the deck they support — the bankZones
bug's twin, found by a finder who had never seen it.

### The R2 wall-clamp bypass

`collisions-deep` failed twice, and the barrier fix was the obvious suspect. It
was exonerated by measurement — the physics wall is the `markBarrier` lateral
table, bit-identical between base and integration on monza/monaco/jeddah — and
the real mechanism is **pre-existing and latent**: a wall hit of severity ≥34
notifies `IncidentSim`, which takes ownership of the car, and the ownership
check in `js/game.js` then **skips the wall clamp**, so the player tumbles
through the barrier. Player-only, RNG-free, untouched by any merge, and
confirmed 2/2 red at base. The spec now isolates the clamp via the sanctioned
`__apex.incident` flags-off path (`65c50637`) with its assertions untouched; the
product question — *should the clamp remain an outer bound during an R2
takeover?* — is a continuation item, not a test fix.

### The props-over-road reading was the test, not the track

Two grandstands (cota `f=0.88`, indianapolis `f=0.33`) reported ~4.7 m of
geometry over the racing line. Measured against the corridor sample set with a
hull test, **cota clears by 14.5–17.8 m and indianapolis by 15.9–19.2 m**. The
reading comes from the spec's own prefilter: a long box laid diagonally has a
world AABB the corridor passes through at a corner, and the box's near-vertical
side faces project in XZ to a degenerate sliver, where the point-in-triangle
test returns `u=v=0` and reports the face's own height as the clearance at any
distance. The `1e-9` area guard admits those slivers. Fixed at the threshold
(`de2215fc`, raised to 0.01 = 5×5 cm of projected area); the two stands were
separately pulled clear of the corridor anyway (`196f7d0d`).

## Process lessons

**Agent worktrees default to a stale base.** Worktree isolation checked out
`origin/main` — a diverged, unrelated history (`f5724152`, an 8,409-line
`game.js`). **All eight** first-round fixer worktrees started there; one
game.js fix had to be redone entirely on the right base, and only the fix's
*shape* transferred. One agent self-corrected unprompted. Remedy: pre-create
worktrees from HEAD, or name the base SHA in the brief and have the agent
`git checkout -B <branch> <sha>` before reading anything.

**`GROUPS` is readonly in bash.** A queue driver that assigns it dies on
assignment. Name the variable anything else.

**A two-group `pick-tests` batch is two Playwright processes.** That is
2 processes × 2 workers on 4 cores, and **every** over-budget timeout this
session sat in a browser+browser pair. `CLAUDE.md`'s "ONE Playwright process"
rule and `pick-tests`' batch output disagree, and the batch output wins by
default because it looks like advice. Run browser groups **singly**; pairing is
safe only for browser+node.

**`Monitor` caps at 30 minutes and `persistent: true` does not lift it** —
re-confirmed. A lapsed watcher is indistinguishable from a quiet one. Every
Monitor needs a `Bash run_in_background` waiter on a completion marker behind it.

**A session limit is survivable if the workflow journals.** W4's round 2
produced 21 candidates and then died in verification — no `confirmed[]`, nothing
in the record. It was recovered out-of-band by harvesting the journal and
running a single verifier at the same adversarial standard: 20 of 21 survived
(1 refuted on its own evidence, 2 kept with corrected evidence), the three
never-run lenses yielded 2 more, and the hot-path allocation lens returned an
honest empty. **+22 findings that would otherwise have been lost.**

**The challenge layer paid for itself — four times, plainly.** Every fix went to
an independent reviewer whose default was to refute, and the reviews were not
ceremony:

- **agentview** — the first wrap fix regressed **full-lap spans** (`s0=0→s1=1`,
  monaco/montreal/redbull ×8 registrations) to `lengthM` 0, by copying a sibling
  that had the same latent bug. The reviewer found `along()`'s `|| n` as the
  ground truth and sent it back; the merged fix carries `|| 1` at **both** sites
  plus a three-case pin, and its mutants are verified killed.
- **net** — the first gate covered the post-grid path, which is nearly dead. The
  reviewer traced the **live** quali flow through `lobby.js`'s pre-hand-off
  `onEvent` handlers and showed a guest could still overwrite the **host's own**
  lap. That is where the second commit came from.
- **Bedrock Ph0** — the registry's `TrackDefs` count cap was a ratchet trap: it
  would have failed on the next circuit added. Now a location rule
  (`^js/circuits/`) with no count cap — a rogue writer still fails.
- **the characterization golden** — a regenerated baseline is the easiest thing
  in the repo to wave through. It was held to a per-scenario diff instead, and
  the two scenarios that should not have moved are byte-identical.

## The do-not list, re-affirmed

The re-decision panel re-opened all six of these at the user's explicit request
and upheld every one on fresh measurement. They are settled; re-opening them
again needs a new *fact*, not a new opinion.

- **Do not split `index.html`.** The "~1,390 static DOM nodes" premise
  reproduces under no parse method (952 body elements / 1,133 by the rule's own
  grep), partials cost an RTT per screen and break the `sw.js` precache
  derivation, and generated sections destroy the "grep index.html is the truth"
  property that `load-order.test.mjs` depends on.
- **Do not merge screens.** 20 screens / 25 layers, not 38–40; merging converts
  free platform behaviour (`showModal()` inertness, Escape, per-dialog focus
  scoping) into hand-rolled mode state.
- **Do not rename the grandfathered `js/game/` filenames.** Same 46 files, same
  contents, a five-point lockstep each. Earliest rational reconsideration is
  after `gen-manifest` lands.
- **Do not split the megablocks.** `updateCar()` (~1,190 ln) and `render()`
  (~1,413 ln) stay fenced until a per-step characterization harness exists —
  and none is scheduled.
- **Do not re-split `css/`.** The 543-class count is invariant under any file
  re-boundary; `@layer` is already live in all 11 files.
- **Do not move `js/` directories.** The re-open trigger is Bedrock Phase 2
  landing, or the first observed navigation/CI-routing failure — neither has
  happened.

Two more from this session's own evidence:

- **Do not flip a curvature sign by grep.** Still open, still requiring a
  rendered lap that shows kerbs and barriers on the wrong side. The barrier fix
  this session was deliberately **vertical only** for exactly this reason.
- **Do not widen a tolerance to clear a red.** Every red this session resolved
  to an attribution: a stale frame, a harness artifact, a genuine pre-existing
  defect, or a deliberate product question. None of them wanted a bigger number.

## Agent-infrastructure hardening (df5716ea)

Applied from the token-efficiency research pass (sources: aakashx.com
"Parallel Claude Code Agents", 2026-05-16; tylerfolkman.substack.com "Claude
Skills Solve the Context Window Problem"):

- **PreToolUse protection hook** (`.claude/hooks/protect-worktree-files.sh`,
  registered in `.claude/settings.json`): blocks Write/Edit of
  shared-contract files (index.html, manifest.cjs, version.json, sw.js,
