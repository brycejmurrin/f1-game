New: Optional helmet visor under Settings → HUD & Camera → Cockpit. Choose CLEAR or TINTED, with Neutral, Amber, Ice Blue or Rose lenses at Light, Medium or Strong intensity. Settings persist and default to OFF.
Improved: Helmet shields now offer independent light refraction (Off/Light/Medium/Strong), reflections (Off/Light/Medium/Strong), rain amount (Off/Light/Normal/Heavy) and runoff (Slow/Natural/Fast). Edge refraction displaces the live world while leaving HUD text clear. Water beads gather and slide downward at low speed, form predominantly sideways streaks at racing speed, and drain after a shower ends. Pause freezes water; reduced motion keeps stationary fading beads. Refraction is an artistic screen-space approximation, not physical ray tracing.
Improved: Visor water now uses small, asymmetric translucent beads instead of bright circular bubbles. Gravity pulls stopped beads straight down; speed-squared airflow around the curved shield increases sideways runoff and produces narrow streaks. The animated Visor Lab restores an accessible A/B wipe with matched original and selected views; its HTML, styling, export helper and original captures are included in the source tree.
Improved: Visor refraction now bends a wider part of the shield and reflection levels add visible coated-lens highlights. The Visor Lab offers a larger viewer with collapsible settings; wet-weather comparisons are captured with verified surface wetness rather than during its initial ramp.
New: Reusable A/B capture tool captures matching frozen game views from JSON recipes or imports arbitrary equal-size screenshot pairs.
