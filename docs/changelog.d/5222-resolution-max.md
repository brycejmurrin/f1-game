New: RESOLUTION has a new MAX setting (three.js renderer) that draws the 3D view at your screen's full pixel density instead of the phone's 1.5× cap, so a 3× iPhone gets the whole panel instead of half of it per axis. It is opt-in: AUTO, LOW, MED and HIGH draw what they did before, and MAX drops back to HIGH's sharpness by itself the first time the frame rate slips or the phone heats up (#5222).
