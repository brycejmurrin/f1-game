Fixed: Simulated qualifying now matches how the AI really drives. A lap at the AI's pace no longer takes pole by seconds (Monza and Spa were the worst): drive exactly as well as the AI and you qualify around the middle of the grid, at every circuit; the front takes a lap clearly quicker than theirs. The simulated grid is also spread out the way the cars really are (about four seconds from first to last, not one), because each team's wing level now counts in the simulated lap. Known effect in VS FRIEND qualifying: a player who taps SIMULATE gets the simulated time, which is now quicker than before; that is being fixed separately (#5783).
Fixed: Time trial and daily challenge medal targets are quicker at every circuit and now sit just ahead of the AI's own best lap. Time trial medals you have already won are kept; an old daily challenge best that you share again is graded against the new target and can show a lower medal (#5783).
Fixed: A qualifying lap you already drove in a saved weekend keeps its grid; a qualifying you only simulated is drawn again with the new times when you come back to it (#5783).
Improved: With tyre wear on, the AI now prices a pit stop against its real lap time, so a stop costs it a little more of a lap than before. Most races are unchanged; at high-wear circuits such as the Red Bull Ring some AI cars now plan one stop where they planned two, and the order they use their tyres in can change (#5783).
