New: In Settings → Controls → Touch Button Response, tune Left, Right, Gas and Brake independently. Choose how each press starts, how far to drag and whether to show its input level. Left and Right can each follow the global Adaptive Buttons setting or use their own steering response at speed. Gas and Brake can each ease in when you slide onto them. Reset restores the selected button alone. On-button feedback lights the whole button as input builds. Three rising bars identify a progressive button before the first tap. The modern and retro cockpit wheels show continuous throttle and brake demand, including light initial presses.
