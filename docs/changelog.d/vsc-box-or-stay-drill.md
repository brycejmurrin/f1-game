New: LEARN's VSC Pit Strategy lesson has a race drill, VSC: BOX OR STAY. A Virtual Safety Car is called just before the pit entry in a 12-lap Red Bull Ring race. The AI drives your car and you make the call: BOX or STAY OUT. The game then runs both calls to the flag from the same moment and shows what each one was worth. There are three rounds (a fresh set, a stop that was due anyway, a worn set near the end), and each right call counts toward the medal (#6559).
