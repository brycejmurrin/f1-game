New: LEARN's Monza Aero Drill now ends with a result card: a bronze, silver or gold medal for the metres you close on the rival in its wake, with RETRY and BACK TO LESSON. BACK TO LESSON reopens the Aero Lab with your run shown beside the diagram. A RETRY chip on the guide card restarts the run without the pause menu. Medals are saved on this device and shown on the LEARN index. They never pay career money.
