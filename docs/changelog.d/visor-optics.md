Improved: the HELMET camera's visor is a real eye-port (flat brow, rounded lower corners, cheek pads) instead of an ellipse vignette; its reflections are added light that follows the scene — the sun's glint and rim sheen slide with your heading, floodlights, street lamps and tail-lights glint where they are, and a dark scene adds nothing; VISOR REFRACTION bends the rim only and adds a chromatic fringe.
